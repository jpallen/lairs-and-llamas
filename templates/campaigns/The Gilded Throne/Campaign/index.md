# DM Guide

## Overview

The prosperous trade city of Ashenmere has long been governed by its Council of Guilds — five powerful merchant houses that balance each other's ambitions. But when the beloved Guildmaster of the Weavers' Guild is found dead under mysterious circumstances, a charismatic newcomer named **Vaelith Sorn** rises to fill the power vacuum, uniting the frightened citizens behind promises of security and reform. What no one suspects is that Sorn is a **rakshasa** — a fiend of ancient cunning who has spent decades cultivating a network of spies, debt-holders, and charmed officials across the Sword Coast.

As the party arrives in Ashenmere seeking work, they are drawn into a web of guild rivalries, disappeared dissidents, and strange new laws that slowly tighten around the city like a noose. By the time the truth becomes clear, Sorn's influence has spread far beyond the council chamber — and removing a creature that cannot truly die will require more than steel.

The campaign runs from **level 1 to 11** using milestone levelling. Characters level up at story beats, not quest counts, so a party that skips optional quests will still progress — but will have fewer allies, less intelligence, and harder fights.

## The City of Ashenmere

Ashenmere is a mid-sized trade city on a navigable river, prosperous but politically fragile. It is governed by the **Council of Guilds** — five merchant houses that share power through uneasy consensus. The city is divided into distinct quarters:

- **The Docks** — Brokka Ironjaw's domain. Warehouses, fishmongers, the harbour, and the Dockmasters' Guild hall. Rough but honest.
- **The Merchants' Quarter** — The wealthiest district. Thornwall Manor, the Goldsmiths' Guild, high-end shops, and the Grand Theatre.
- **The Tanners' Quarter** — Industrial and working-class. The Tanners' Guild, tanneries, dyers, and the abandoned building that hides Sorn's alchemical lab.
- **The Old Quarter** — The oldest part of the city. Brother Aldous's temple of Tyr, narrow streets, the Silent Ward, and entrances to the undercity.
- **The Council Citadel** — A fortified government building at the city's centre. Sorn takes it as his seat of power and eventually fortifies it with fiendish wards.
- **The Weavers' District** — Maren Loomwright's home territory. Textile workshops, the resistance's early safehouses, and the Ashenmere Broadsheet office.

Between quests, encourage players to explore freely. They can visit markets, taverns, guild halls, and temples. Describe the city as vibrant but uneasy — rumours of the Weavers' Guildmaster's death, rising prices, and strangers asking too many questions.

## The Villain: Vaelith Sorn

Sorn is a rakshasa who was exiled from the court of Mahavan, a powerful rakshasa rajah in the Nine Hells, after a failed bid for power. Stripped of his standing and cast out to the Material Plane, Sorn has spent decades rebuilding — not merely to survive, but to return to Mahavan's court with an army at his back and a mortal city as his throne.

Ashenmere is not just a prize; it is a proving ground. The summoning portal he is constructing will allow him to pull loyal fiends through from the Nine Hells, establishing a beachhead on the Material Plane that even Mahavan cannot ignore.

**Personality:** Vain, theatrical, and deeply resentful of those who dismissed him. He sees mortals as pieces on a game board, but his need for admiration is genuine — he craves the adoration of Ashenmere's citizens almost as much as he craves power.

**Weakness:** His vanity. He cannot resist showing off, taking credit, and lingering to savour his victories. Clever players can exploit this.

**Methods:** Charm magic, blackmail, doppelganger agents, political manipulation. He avoids direct violence until forced.

**Presence:** The party should encounter Sorn socially at least 2-3 times before the finale — at the Guild Trials (Q4), the Masquerade (Q8), and during the Civil Unrest (Q11). He is always polished, charming, and dismissive of the party until they become a real threat.

## Key NPCs

### The Council of Guilds

- **Maren Loomwright** (Weavers' Guild) — Daughter of the murdered Guildmaster Thera. Young, fierce, and politically inexperienced but deeply motivated. She inherits her mother's council seat but lacks votes to challenge Sorn. Leads the underground resistance. Accompanies the party on key missions.
- **Brokka Ironjaw** (Dockmasters' Guild) — Gruff half-orc harbourmaster. Pragmatic and loyal to those who prove themselves. The first guild leader to support the party. Sorn's trade regulations threaten her livelihood directly.
- **Lady Isara Thornwall** (Merchants' Guild) — Aristocratic human socialite. Publicly neutral, privately terrified — Sorn has blackmail material on her family. Can be turned into an ally if the party frees her from his leverage.
- **Guildmaster Harsk Copperkettle** (Tanners' Guild) — Elderly dwarf, cautious and suspicious. Survived decades of guild politics by never committing to a side. Convincing him requires hard evidence, not promises.
- **Guildmaster Selene Auris** (Goldsmiths' Guild) — Ambitious human woman who sees Sorn as a useful ally — for now. Not charmed or blackmailed; she genuinely believes his reforms benefit her guild. Can only be turned against him with undeniable proof.

### Other Key NPCs

- **Brother Aldous** (Church of Tyr) — An aging cleric who runs a small, struggling temple in the Old Quarter. Appears as a minor NPC in Act I offering healing and quiet warnings. Becomes plot-critical in Quest 10 when rescued, confirming Sorn's true nature.
- **Vara Inkwell** (Journalist) — Sharp-tongued halfling who runs the Ashenmere Broadsheet. A thorn in Sorn's side. Central to Quest 9.
- **The Valiant Company** (Rival Adventurers) — A competing adventuring party: Dren (human fighter, cocky), Sylva (elf ranger, observant), Wick (gnome wizard, nervous), and Patches (tiefling rogue, irreverent). Friendly rivals who can be recruited as allies in Act IV.

## Levelling

Characters level up at **story milestones**, not by completing a set number of quests:

| Level | Milestone |
|-------|-----------|
| 2 | Survive the funeral attack and ally with Maren |
| 3 | Uncover evidence of Sorn's network (dossier, doppelganger, or smuggled goods) |
| 4 | Complete any two quests in Ashenmere after joining the resistance |
| 5 | Complete any two more quests, OR discover the charm amulets AND the undercity prison |
| 6 | Obtain solid evidence of Sorn's corruption (masquerade letters, journalist's evidence, or hag's testimony) |
| 7 | Learn Sorn's true nature (rescue Brother Aldous, or piece together enough clues) |
| 8 | Survive the civil unrest AND obtain a means to slay a rakshasa |
| 9 | Build a resistance coalition strong enough to challenge the Citadel (at least 3 allied factions) |
| 10 | Destroy the ward stones and breach the Citadel |
| 11 | Reach the inner sanctum and disrupt the summoning ritual |

## Sorn's Escalation Track

Track how many quests the party has completed that directly oppose Sorn. Use this to pace his reactions:

| Anti-Sorn Quests | Sorn's Response |
|------------------|-----------------|
| 1-2 | Sorn considers the party minor irritants. Sends agents to observe (imps, spies). |
| 3-4 | Sorn takes action against allies. **Q9 triggers** (Vara framed). Doppelganger surveillance increases. |
| 5-6 | Sorn views the party as a serious threat. **Q11 triggers** (martial law). City watch hunts resistance. |
| 7+ | Sorn activates contingencies. **Q13 triggers** (traitor). Fortifies the Citadel, accelerates the ritual. |
| Alliance-building | **Q16 triggers** (retribution strike). Sorn tries to destroy the resistance. |

Anti-Sorn quests: Q3, Q5, Q6 (if siding against Merchants), Q7, Q8, Q9, Q10, Q14. Also count any public defiance of Sorn.

## Slaying a Rakshasa

The **Blade of Tyr's Witness** (Quest 12) is the primary means of permanently killing Sorn, but the campaign should never hinge on a single item. Alternative methods:

- **Brother Aldous's Blessing** — Consecrates any piercing weapon for a single strike (1 minute of concentration during the boss fight).
- **The Cursed Cargo** — Purified by a cleric/paladin using a 5th-level spell. Functions as a blessed weapon for 24 hours.
- **The Last Sermon** — If Quest 14 was completed, Aldous consecrated a backup weapon.
- **Portal Reversal** — Notes in the ritual chamber (Quest 20) reveal the portal can be reversed to banish Sorn (DC 20 combined Arcana + Athletics check).
- **Divine Intervention** — Standard rules for clerics who worship a god of justice.

## Quest Summary

Quests are discovered organically through exploration, NPC relationships, and Sorn's escalating actions — not from a quest board. Each quest is marked as **Story** (required), **Discovery** (optional), or **Reactive** (triggered by events).

### Act I: Welcome to Ashenmere (Levels 1-3)

The party arrives and establishes themselves. The city seems prosperous but cracks are showing.

| Quest | Title | Type | Style | Level |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Q1 | [The Dockside Dispute](quest-01-dockside-dispute.md) | Story | Combat/Social | 1 |
| Q2 | The Funeral Procession | Story | Street chase/Combat | 2 |
| Q3 | Trouble at the Tannery | Discovery | Investigation/Dungeon | 3 |

**Q1: The Dockside Dispute.** The party arrives and witnesses a confrontation on the docks — guild workers refuse to unload a falsified cargo manifest. Hired thugs attack Brokka Ironjaw. A broken crate reveals smuggled alchemical supplies marked with an unfamiliar sigil. The party meets Brokka and gets their first job.

**Q2: The Funeral Procession.** During Guildmaster Thera's public funeral, thugs attack the procession, targeting Maren. A street chase through the city leads to a cornered bandit captain. The party gains Maren as an ally and patron. Brother Aldous is glimpsed among the mourners — their first sighting of a key NPC.

**Q3: Trouble at the Tannery.** Workers are falling ill from poisoned water. Investigating leads to a hidden alchemical lab beneath an abandoned building. The party discovers a blackmail dossier and encounters their first doppelganger. Harsk Copperkettle becomes a cautious ally. *If skipped: no dossier, no doppelganger reveal, Harsk stays neutral.*

### Act II: The Web Tightens (Levels 4-6)

Sorn is elected to the council. New laws restrict movement. Dissidents begin vanishing. The party transitions from hired muscle to active investigators.

| Quest | Title | Type | Style | Level |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Q4 | Guild Trials | Discovery | Social/Skill challenge | 4 |
| Q5 | The Silent Ward | Discovery | Horror investigation | 4 |
| Q6 | Warehouse Wars | Discovery | Faction combat | 5 |
| Q7 | The Disappeared | Story | Stealth dungeon crawl | 5 |
| Q8 | Masquerade at Thornwall Manor | Discovery | Heist/Infiltration | 6 |
| Q9 | The Poisoned Quill | Reactive | Noir investigation | 6 |

**Q4: Guild Trials.** A public contest of skill — combat tourney, riddle challenge, rooftop race, negotiation contest. The party meets the Valiant Company and all five guild leaders. A competitor is caught cheating with a charm amulet — the first hint of Sorn's enchantment network. *If skipped: harder to recruit the Valiant Company and Selene Auris later.*

**Q5: The Silent Ward.** An entire neighbourhood has gone quiet. A night hag torments residents in their dreams, extracting secrets she sells to Sorn. The party enters the Ethereal Border to confront her. She can be bargained with — revealing Sorn has "a face that isn't his own" — or killed, freeing the ward permanently. *If skipped: no shapechanger intelligence, harder doppelganger encounters later.*

**Q6: Warehouse Wars.** Sorn's trade regulations are squeezing the Dockmasters. Brokka asks the party to protect a contraband shipment. The Merchants' Guild sends enforcers. A morally grey faction conflict that forces the party to pick a side — or broker peace. Consequences ripple through the rest of the campaign.

**Q7: The Disappeared.** Maren's resistance contacts are vanishing. She accompanies the party through the undercity to find a secret prison run by Sorn's loyalists. Stealth-focused dungeon crawl. Rescued prisoners reveal Sorn speaking Infernal and mutter about "the tiger that walks like a man."

**Q8: Masquerade at Thornwall Manor.** The party infiltrates a lavish masquerade ball to steal correspondence proving Sorn blackmails council members. Heist mechanics — disguises, deception, navigating guards. Combat is a failure state. The letters reference "the shipment from Ul-Razzam" — connecting to the cursed cargo. *If skipped: no blackmail evidence, Lady Thornwall stays under Sorn's control.*

**Q9: The Poisoned Quill.** Vara Inkwell has been framed for murder. The party must prove her innocence before her trial at dawn. Ticking clock, enchanted witnesses, planted evidence, and a hired assassin sent to silence her. If saved, Vara's broadsheets erode Sorn's public support. *If failed: Vara is executed, hostile civilians in Act III.*

### Act III: Unmasking the Fiend (Levels 7-8)

The party knows the scope of the conspiracy but not Sorn's true nature. This act is about uncovering what he really is and finding the means to stop him.

| Quest | Title | Type | Style | Level |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Q10 | The Cursed Cargo | Story | Ship assault | 7 |
| Q11 | Civil Unrest | Reactive | Large-scale urban combat | 7 |
| Q12 | The Old Temple | Discovery | Temple dungeon | 8 |
| Q13 | The Traitor Unmasked | Reactive | Social deduction | 8 |
| Q14 | The Last Sermon | Discovery | Defence encounter | 8 |

**Q10: The Cursed Cargo.** The party intercepts a ship carrying cursed weapons and a prisoner: Brother Aldous. Rescuing him confirms Sorn is a rakshasa — immune to spells of 6th level or lower, killable only with a blessed piercing weapon. Aldous knows where one might be found.

**Q11: Civil Unrest.** Sorn declares martial law after a staged attack. The city erupts. The party must navigate urban combat, protecting civilians and rallying resistance fighters. During the battle, Sorn's disguise briefly flickers, revealing tiger-like features. The secret is out.

**Q12: The Old Temple.** A ruined temple of Tyr in the hills holds the **Blade of Tyr's Witness** — a blessed weapon that can permanently slay a rakshasa. Temple dungeon with puzzle rooms, divine trials, and a bone devil boss guarding the crypt. *If skipped: no Blade, must rely on alternative methods.*

**Q13: The Traitor Unmasked.** A resistance member is secretly a doppelganger. After a safehouse is raided, the party must identify the traitor through social deduction and investigation. Ends with a confrontation and a backup assassin.

**Q14: The Last Sermon.** Brother Aldous delivers a public sermon denouncing Sorn. The party defends him against waves of attackers for 10 rounds. If successful, Aldous consecrates a backup blessed weapon and the Church of Tyr rallies. *If skipped: no backup weapon, no divine support.*

### Act IV: The Gilded Throne (Levels 9-11)

Sorn retreats to the Council Citadel. The party must rally allies, break through his defences, and kill the rakshasa before he opens a permanent gate to the Nine Hells.

| Quest | Title | Type | Style | Level |
|-------|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Q15 | The Outlander Alliance | Story | Diplomacy/Wilderness | 9 |
| Q16 | Sorn's Retribution | Reactive | Defensive siege | 9 |
| Q17 | Sabotage | Story | Triple mini-encounter | 9 |
| Q18 | The Siege of the Citadel | Story | Dungeon assault | 10 |
| Q19 | The Devil's Vanguard | Story | Mini-boss combat | 10 |
| Q20 | The Ritual Chamber | Story | Combat/Puzzle hybrid | 10 |
| Q21 | The Pretender's Court | Story | Roleplay/Psychological | 11 |
| Q22 | The True Face | Story | Multi-phase boss battle | 11 |

**Q15: The Outlander Alliance.** The party travels outside the city to forge alliances. Available allies depend on earlier choices — cultivated guild leaders, the Ironholt Dwarves, Thornwood Druids, the Valiant Company, and Selene Auris (hardest to turn). Each ally has a price or task.

**Q16: Sorn's Retribution.** Sorn sends a coordinated strike team against the resistance headquarters. Three waves — veterans, barbed devils, then an assassin targeting Maren. A test of the party's defensive planning.

**Q17: Sabotage.** Three ward stones protect the Citadel. Each must be destroyed in a single night, each with a different approach: the Flooded Sewer (brute combat vs a hezrou), the Bell Tower (stealth past chain devils), and the Grand Theatre (social deception around a succubus).

**Q18: The Siege of the Citadel.** The resistance assaults the Citadel. The party leads a strike team inside via one of three routes: front gate (hard combat, ally support), servant's passage (stealth), or sewers (environmental hazards). Routes converge at a cambion mid-boss.

**Q19: The Devil's Vanguard.** A horned devil tears through the Citadel walls, rampaging through the courtyard. A straightforward mini-boss fight to save the allied forces.

**Q20: The Ritual Chamber.** The summoning portal blazes below the throne room. Four anchor points sustain the ritual, each requiring a different method to disrupt (strength, arcana, divine magic, and brute destruction). An erinyes commands the defence.

**Q21: The Pretender's Court.** Sorn has created a mockery of a royal court in the throne room. He offers the party a final bargain, addressing each member's fears and desires. Rakshasa spawn disguised as courtiers attack when the party refuses.

**Q22: The True Face.** Multi-phase boss battle against Vaelith Sorn (enhanced rakshasa, 200 HP, legendary and lair actions). Phase 1: illusions and charm. Phase 2: fury and flight, hellfire lair actions. Phase 3: the portal collapses, pulling everything toward it. A blessed weapon must deliver the killing blow.

## How the Plot Hangs Together

### The Thread of Evidence

The campaign's central mystery — *who is Sorn?* — unfolds through accumulating evidence:

1. **The Sigil** (Q1) — An unfamiliar mark on smuggled goods. Appears again on the cursed cargo (Q10).
2. **Calishite Coin** (Q1, Q2) — Sorn's agents are paid in foreign coin that keeps turning up.
3. **Charm Amulets** (Q4, Q9) — Enchantment devices used to control agents. Collecting multiple provides evidence of a coordinated network.
4. **The Doppelgangers** (Q3, Q5, Q9, Q13) — Shapechanging agents throughout the city. The hag (Q5) provides early warning; the tannery (Q3) provides the first encounter.
5. **Infernal Speech** (Q7) — A prisoner hears Sorn speaking Infernal.
6. **"The Tiger That Walks Like a Man"** (Q7) — A mentally broken prisoner's mutterings.
7. **The Hag's Warning** (Q5) — "He has a face that isn't his own."
8. **The Blackmail Letters** (Q8) — Prove Sorn controls the council through coercion.
9. **Brother Aldous's Confirmation** (Q10) — The first definitive identification: Sorn is a rakshasa.
10. **The Flickering Mask** (Q11) — The public sees Sorn's true face for the first time.

### Paths Through the Campaign

The campaign is not linear. At each stage, the party chooses which threads to pursue:

**Act I** is mostly sequential — Q1 introduces the setting, Q2 introduces Maren, and Q3 is the first optional thread.

**Act II** opens up. Q4-Q6 are available concurrently. Q7 advances the main plot. Q8 and Q9 can happen in either order, and Q9 is reactive (triggered by Sorn's escalation track, not player choice).

**Act III** is the most open. Q10 is the pivotal story quest, but Q12, Q13, and Q14 can be pursued in any order once the party knows Sorn is a rakshasa. Q11 erupts when the escalation track triggers it.

**Act IV** is linear by necessity — the siege cascades from Q17 through Q22 as a continuous operation. Q15 and Q16 are the preparation phase.

### Key Items That Connect Quests

Several items found in early quests have optional but significant effects on later quests:

- **Heartstone** (Q5) — Detects fiends within 30 ft. Useful for spotting doppelgangers in Q9/Q13.
- **Charm Amulets** (Q4, Q9) — Accumulate as evidence to convince sceptical NPCs.
- **Thornwall Manor Journal** (Q8) — Contains a map to the Old Temple (Q12).
- **Brass Compass** (Q7) — Points toward the ward stones (Q17).
- **Sending Stones** (Q7) — Communication with Maren throughout the campaign.
- **Wick's Arcane Notes** (Q4) — Advantage on detecting Sorn's magic.
- **Vara's Coded Message** (Q9) — Names of Sorn's agents, helps identify the traitor (Q13).
- **Ward Stone Shard** (Q16) — Locates the remaining ward stones (Q17).
- **Stone of Far Speech** (Q14) — Feed Sorn false information in Act IV.
- **Doppelganger's Journal** (Q13) — Reveals ritual anchor weak points (Q20).
- **Portal Reversal Notes** (Q20) — Failsafe method to defeat Sorn without the Blade.
