# The Gilded Throne

## Overview

The prosperous trade city of Ashenmere has long been governed by its Council of Guilds — five powerful merchant houses that balance each other's ambitions. But when the beloved Guildmaster of the Weavers' Guild is found dead under mysterious circumstances, a charismatic newcomer named **Vaelith Sorn** rises to fill the power vacuum, uniting the frightened citizens behind promises of security and reform. What no one suspects is that Sorn is a **rakshasa** — a fiend of ancient cunning who has spent decades cultivating a network of spies, debt-holders, and charmed officials across the Sword Coast. As the party arrives in Ashenmere seeking work, they are drawn into a web of guild rivalries, disappeared dissidents, and strange new laws that slowly tighten around the city like a noose. By the time the truth becomes clear, Sorn's influence has spread far beyond the council chamber — and removing a creature that cannot truly die will require more than steel.

## The Villain: Vaelith Sorn

Sorn is a rakshasa who was exiled from the court of Mahavan, a powerful rakshasa rajah in the Nine Hells, after a failed bid for power. Stripped of his standing and cast out to the Material Plane, Sorn has spent decades rebuilding — not merely to survive, but to return to Mahavan's court with an army at his back and a mortal city as his throne. Ashenmere is not just a prize; it is a proving ground. The summoning portal he is constructing will allow him to pull loyal fiends through from the Nine Hells, establishing a beachhead on the Material Plane that even Mahavan cannot ignore. Sorn is vain, theatrical, and deeply resentful of those who dismissed him. He sees mortals as pieces on a game board, but his need for admiration is genuine — he craves the adoration of Ashenmere's citizens almost as much as he craves power. This vanity is his weakness: he cannot resist showing off, taking credit, and lingering to savour his victories.

## Key NPCs

### The Council of Guilds

- **Maren Loomwright** (Weavers' Guild) — Daughter of the murdered Guildmaster Thera. Young, fierce, and politically inexperienced but deeply motivated. She inherits her mother's council seat but lacks votes to challenge Sorn. Leads the underground resistance. Accompanies the party on key missions (Quests 7, 8, 11).
- **Brokka Ironjaw** (Dockmasters' Guild) — Gruff half-orc harbourmaster. Pragmatic and loyal to those who prove themselves. The first guild leader to support the party. Sorn's trade regulations threaten her livelihood directly.
- **Lady Isara Thornwall** (Merchants' Guild) — Aristocratic human socialite. Publicly neutral, privately terrified — Sorn has blackmail material on her family. Can be turned into an ally if the party frees her from Sorn's leverage.
- **Guildmaster Harsk Copperkettle** (Tanners' Guild) — Elderly dwarf, cautious and suspicious. He has survived decades of guild politics by never committing to a side. Convincing him requires hard evidence, not promises. The tannery poisoning (Quest 3) directly threatens his people.
- **Guildmaster Selene Auris** (Goldsmiths' Guild) — Ambitious human woman who sees Sorn as a useful ally — for now. She is not charmed or blackmailed; she genuinely believes Sorn's reforms benefit her guild. She can be turned against him only when confronted with undeniable proof of his fiendish nature.

### Other Key NPCs

- **Brother Aldous** (Church of Tyr) — An aging cleric who runs a small, struggling temple in the Old Quarter. He appears as a minor NPC in Act I (the party can visit the temple during downtime, and he offers healing and quiet warnings about "something wrong in this city"). He becomes plot-critical in Quest 10 when rescued from the cursed cargo ship, confirming Sorn's true nature. The Church of Tyr is fading because Sorn has been quietly diverting city funds away from temples and encouraging secular "reform" — part of his campaign to weaken divine influence that could threaten him.
- **Vara Inkwell** (Journalist) — Sharp-tongued halfling who runs the Ashenmere Broadsheet. A thorn in Sorn's side. Central to Quest 9.
- **The Valiant Company** (Rival Adventurers) — A competing adventuring party: Dren (human fighter, cocky), Sylva (elf ranger, observant), Wick (gnome wizard, nervous), and Patches (tiefling rogue, irreverent). Met during the Guild Trials, they recur as friendly rivals and can be recruited as allies in Act IV.

## Levelling

The campaign runs from level 1 to 11. Characters level up at **story milestones**, not by completing a set number of quests. The milestones are:

| Level | Milestone |
|-------|-----------|
| 2 | Survive the funeral attack and ally with Maren |
| 3 | Uncover evidence of Sorn's network (dossier, doppelganger, or smuggled goods) |
| 4 | Complete any two quests in Ashenmere after joining the resistance |
| 5 | Complete any two more quests, OR discover the charm amulets AND the undercity prison |
| 6 | Obtain solid evidence of Sorn's corruption (masquerade letters, journalist's evidence, or hag's testimony) |
| 7 | Learn Sorn's true nature (rescue Brother Aldous, or piece together enough clues: Infernal speech, "tiger that walks like a man," charm amulets, doppelganger agents) |
| 8 | Survive the civil unrest AND obtain a means to slay a rakshasa |
| 9 | Build a resistance coalition strong enough to challenge the Citadel (at least 3 allied factions) |
| 10 | Destroy the ward stones and breach the Citadel |
| 11 | Reach the inner sanctum and disrupt the summoning ritual |

This means a party that skips optional quests will still progress, but will have fewer allies, less intelligence, and harder fights. A thorough party that pursues every thread will be better prepared for the finale.

## Quest Structure

Quests are not assigned from a quest board. They are discovered through exploration, NPC relationships, and Sorn's escalating actions. Each quest has a **trigger** (how the party learns about it) and a **type**:

- **Story** — Advances the main plot. Cannot be skipped without derailing the campaign.
- **Discovery** — Found through investigation, exploration, or NPC relationships. Optional but rewarding.
- **Reactive** — Triggered by Sorn's actions or story events. Happens TO the party whether they're ready or not.

### Quest Overview

Each quest lists multiple discovery paths. The party only needs ONE to find the quest. The more paths the party has uncovered, the more prepared they'll be when they arrive.

**Q1: The Dockside Dispute** (Story)
- The party witnesses it on arrival — unavoidable if they enter through the docks
- If arriving by road, Brokka's workers are arguing at the city gate about a diverted shipment, leading to the same confrontation

**Q2: The Funeral Procession** (Story)
- Brokka mentions it and invites the party as thanks for Q1
- Public event — funeral notices are posted around the city; any innkeeper or shopkeeper mentions it
- Brother Aldous mentions it if the party visits the temple
- The party hears the funeral bells and follows the crowd

**Q3: Trouble at the Tannery** (Discovery — optional)
- Harsk Copperkettle hears about the party and asks for help directly
- The party notices sick workers while exploring the Tanners' Quarter
- A tanner collapses in the street or at the inn where the party is staying
- Maren mentions the tannery illness as a possible lead on Sorn's operations
- **If skipped:** The party misses the blackmail dossier and the doppelganger reveal. Harsk remains neutral and cannot be recruited in Act IV.

**Q4: Guild Trials** (Discovery — optional)
- Posters around the city advertise the trials — impossible to miss while exploring
- Brokka or any guild contact suggests entering to build reputation
- The Valiant Company challenges the party after meeting them at an inn or tavern
- A merchant offers to sponsor the party's entry in exchange for a favour
- **If skipped:** The party never meets the Valiant Company or Selene Auris socially. Recruiting either later requires harder persuasion.

**Q5: The Silent Ward** (Discovery — optional)
- The party notices a quiet neighbourhood while exploring — shops shuttered, streets empty
- Maren mentions a resistance contact in the ward has gone silent
- An innkeeper warns the party to avoid "the quiet streets near the Old Quarter"
- Brother Aldous mentions parishioners from the ward have stopped attending services
- A frightened child from the ward approaches the party begging for help, saying "the bad dreams won't stop"
- **If skipped:** No intelligence about Sorn's shapechangers. Doppelganger encounters in Q9 and Q13 are harder. The ward residents don't shelter the resistance.

**Q6: Warehouse Wars** (Discovery — optional)
- Brokka asks the party directly — she needs trusted muscle
- The party stumbles onto the dock confrontation while exploring at night
- Dock workers the party befriended in Q1 mention Brokka is planning something dangerous
- Vara Inkwell tips the party off — she's heard the Merchants' Guild is planning an enforcement action
- The party overhears Merchants' Guild enforcers discussing the operation at a tavern
- **If skipped:** The guild conflict escalates without the party. Brokka is weakened; Dockmasters are less useful as allies. The party misses the chance to influence guild politics.

**Q7: The Disappeared** (Story)
- Maren's contacts vanish; she asks the party for help
- The party notices resistance members going missing on their own
- A freed prisoner from another encounter mentions the undercity prison
- The party follows a suspicious city guard patrol and discovers the prison entrance
- A note is found on a captured Sorn agent mentioning "the cells beneath the Old Foundation"

**Q8: Masquerade at Thornwall Manor** (Discovery — optional)
- Maren learns about the ball through her contacts and suggests infiltration
- The tannery dossier (Q3) names Lady Thornwall as a blackmail target — the party may seek her out
- The hag (Q5) mentions "the woman in the great house who weeps at night" — a hint about Thornwall
- Vara Inkwell mentions the ball in her broadsheet — it's a public society event
- The party spots Sorn's cambion bodyguard entering Thornwall Manor and investigates
- Lady Thornwall herself, if approached in the street or at the Guild Trials, may nervously hint she needs help
- **If skipped:** No blackmail evidence. Lady Thornwall remains under Sorn's control. The cursed cargo must be discovered another way.

**Q9: The Poisoned Quill** (Reactive — can be failed)
- News of Vara's arrest spreads through the city — town criers announce the trial
- Maren sends urgent word to the party
- The party sees Vara being dragged through the streets by the city watch
- A broadsheet boy delivers Vara's last printed pamphlet to the party with a desperate note scrawled on it
- If the party has met Vara previously (through Q6 or general exploration), she sends a message from her cell
- **If ignored or failed:** Vara is convicted and executed. Sorn controls the public narrative. Hostile civilians in Q11 and Q16.

**Q10: The Cursed Cargo** (Story)
- Brother Aldous smuggles a note out of captivity — it reaches the party through the resistance, through a dock worker, or through a parishioner at the temple
- Maren's spies spot the ship entering the harbour flying unfamiliar colours
- The masquerade letters (Q8) mention "the shipment from Ul-Razzam" and a date
- Brokka notices an unlisted vessel docking at a private berth controlled by Sorn's agents
- A charmed sailor stumbles off the ship in a confused state, babbling about "devils in the hold"
- The sigil from Q1 matches markings on cargo being loaded onto a wagon from the harbour

**Q11: Civil Unrest** (Reactive — cannot be avoided)
- Sorn declares martial law after staging a terrorist attack. The city erupts. This triggers automatically once the party has opposed Sorn in 3+ quests.
- The party may accelerate this by publicly confronting Sorn (e.g., at the Guild Trials or the masquerade) or by allowing evidence of their investigation to reach him
- The party may delay this by operating covertly, but Sorn eventually acts regardless — the DM should trigger it when the narrative tension peaks

**Q12: The Old Temple** (Discovery — optional)
- Brother Aldous describes the temple after being rescued (Q10)
- Ancient texts in the Church of Tyr mention "the Tomb of Ser Valen, who slew the serpent with Tyr's own light"
- The tannery dossier (Q3) contains a note: "ensure the paladin's tomb remains sealed" — someone is afraid of what's inside
- A ghost or vision appears to a devout party member in a dream, showing the temple's location
- The hag (Q5), if bargained with, mentions "a weapon that burns fiends sleeps in the hills — the tiger-man fears it"
- The Valiant Company mentions rumours of a haunted temple in the hills that treasure hunters avoid
- **If skipped:** No Blade of Tyr's Witness. The party must rely on alternative means to slay Sorn.

**Q13: The Traitor Unmasked** (Reactive — cannot be avoided)
- A resistance safehouse is raided — the party arrives to find it destroyed
- Resistance members report that Sorn's agents knew their patrol routes and safe words
- The party notices inconsistencies in an NPC's behaviour (the doppelganger slips up)
- Maren mentions that every plan they've shared with the full resistance has been compromised, but plans shared only with the inner circle have not
- An imp is spotted fleeing a resistance meeting — if caught, it reveals the traitor's existence

**Q14: The Last Sermon** (Discovery — optional)
- Brother Aldous proposes the sermon after being rescued and witnessing the civil unrest
- The remaining faithful at the temple ask the party to help Aldous speak publicly
- Maren suggests a public rally to counter Sorn's propaganda — Aldous volunteers
- The party finds a consecration ritual in the temple archives and suggests Aldous perform it publicly for maximum effect
- **If skipped:** No backup blessed weapon. Church of Tyr does not rally. The finale is harder without divine support.

**Q15: The Outlander Alliance** (Story)
- Maren and the party conclude they need outside help — this emerges naturally from planning sessions
- Brokka mentions the Ironholt Dwarves as potential allies
- Brother Aldous knows the Thornwood Druids from before Sorn's rise
- The Valiant Company offer to help recruit if the party asks
- Lady Thornwall (if freed) suggests her household guard and provides intelligence on the Citadel
- Any guild leader the party has cultivated offers their faction's resources when asked

**Q16: Sorn's Retribution** (Reactive — cannot be avoided)
- Sorn sends a strike team after the party begins building alliances
- Can also be triggered if the party is too public about their plans (boasting at taverns, openly recruiting)
- May be foreshadowed by Sorn's agents watching the resistance HQ — perceptive players may notice the surveillance before the attack

**Q17: Sabotage** (Story)
- Captured enemies from Q16 reveal the ward stones under interrogation
- The traitor's intelligence (Q13) includes this information
- Lady Thornwall's guards (if allied) know about the wards from serving in the Citadel
- Brother Aldous senses the wards through divine magic and can locate them
- The party's own investigation of the Citadel's defences (scouting, Arcana checks) reveals the magical barrier and its anchor points
- A defecting city guard approaches the resistance with information about the wards

**Q18-Q22: The Siege and Finale** (Story — sequential)
These cascade from Q17. Once the wards fall, the siege begins at dawn (Q18). The devil's vanguard (Q19) erupts during the assault. The ritual chamber (Q20), pretender's court (Q21), and final battle (Q22) follow as the party pushes deeper into the Citadel. These are not discovered — they are experienced.

### Sorn's Escalation Track

The DM should track how many quests the party has completed that directly oppose Sorn. Use this to pace his reactions:

| Anti-Sorn Quests Completed | Sorn's Response |
|---------------------------|-----------------|
| 1-2 | Sorn is aware of the party but considers them minor irritants. He sends agents to observe (imps, spies). |
| 3-4 | Sorn takes action against the party's allies. **Q9 triggers** (Vara is framed). Doppelganger surveillance increases. |
| 5-6 | Sorn views the party as a serious threat. **Q11 triggers** (martial law). The city watch begins actively hunting resistance members. |
| 7+ | Sorn activates his contingencies. **Q13 triggers** (traitor). He begins fortifying the Citadel and accelerating the ritual. |
| Alliance-building begins | **Q16 triggers** (retribution strike). Sorn tries to destroy the resistance before the party can muster enough force. |

Quests that count as directly opposing Sorn: Q3, Q5, Q6 (if siding against Merchants), Q7, Q8, Q9, Q10, Q14. The DM can also count any party action that publicly defies or undermines Sorn (confronting him at events, spreading counter-propaganda, freeing charmed officials).

## Consequence Chains

Player choices in key quests ripple forward through the campaign:

- **Quest 6 (Warehouse Wars) — Guild Allegiance:** If the party sides with the Dockmasters, Brokka's militia joins the resistance early and provides muscle for Act III, but the Merchants' Guild becomes hostile and Lady Thornwall is harder to recruit in Act IV. If the party sides with the Merchants (or brokers peace), Lady Thornwall is easier to turn but Brokka feels betrayed and must be won back.
- **Quest 9 (The Poisoned Quill) — Journalist's Fate:** If the party saves Vara, her broadsheets erode Sorn's public support — in Act IV, more civilians aid the resistance and fewer guards remain loyal to Sorn. If the party fails, Sorn controls the narrative; the resistance is branded as terrorists, and the party faces hostile civilians during the Civil Unrest (Quest 11).
- **Quest 5 (The Silent Ward) — The Hag's Bargain:** If the party bargains with the night hag, they gain early intelligence about Sorn's shapechanging, making the doppelganger encounters in Quests 9 and 13 easier (the party knows to test for them). If they kill the hag, the intelligence is lost but the ward is permanently freed from her influence, earning the residents' loyalty — they shelter the resistance in Act III.
- **Council Loyalty:** Throughout the campaign, the party's interactions with each guild leader accumulate. In Quest 16 (The Outlander Alliance), the allies available depend partly on which guild leaders the party has cultivated. A party that has ignored or alienated guilds will need to rely more heavily on the outlander factions.

## Slaying a Rakshasa

The **Blade of Tyr's Witness** (Quest 12) is the primary means of permanently killing Sorn, but it is not the only path. Alternative methods the party may discover:

- **Brother Aldous's Blessing:** If Aldous survives to the final battle, he can perform a ritual blessing on any piercing weapon, consecrating it for a single strike. This takes 1 minute of concentration and requires Aldous to be protected during the ritual — a secondary objective during the boss fight.
- **The Cursed Cargo:** The cursed weapons from Quest 10, if purified by a cleric or paladin using a 5th-level spell, can be turned against their maker. A purified weapon functions as a blessed weapon for 24 hours.
- **The Last Sermon:** If the party completed Quest 14 (The Last Sermon), Brother Aldous consecrated a backup weapon during the ritual. This weapon is available as a fallback if the Blade is lost.
- **Divine Intervention:** A cleric in the party who worships a god of justice can attempt Divine Intervention during the final battle, requesting a blessing on their weapon. This follows normal Divine Intervention rules.

The campaign should never hinge on a single item. If the Blade is lost or the party never retrieves it, the DM should ensure at least one alternative is available.

## Act I: Welcome to Ashenmere (Levels 1-3)

The players arrive in the city and establish themselves, taking on guild jobs. The city seems prosperous but cracks are showing — the recent death of Guildmaster Thera Loomwright has everyone on edge, and a smooth-talking newcomer named Vaelith Sorn is rising fast.

**Downtime and Exploration:** Between quests, encourage players to explore Ashenmere freely. They can visit the markets, the docks, the Old Quarter temples (where they first meet Brother Aldous), and the guild halls. Describe the city as vibrant but uneasy — rumours of the Weavers' Guildmaster's death, rising prices, and strangers asking too many questions. These downtime scenes establish the city as a living place and plant seeds for later quests. Provide a brief city gazetteer listing key locations the party can visit at any time.

### Quest 1: The Dockside Dispute (Level 1)

**Overview:** The party arrives in Ashenmere by road or river and immediately witnesses a confrontation on the docks: Dockmasters' Guild workers are refusing to unload a shipment, claiming the cargo manifest has been falsified. A Merchants' Guild clerk insists the papers are in order. Brokka Ironjaw herself is on scene, arguing loudly. Before the dispute can be resolved, hired thugs emerge from the crowd and attack — targeting Brokka. In the chaos, the disputed cargo crate breaks open, revealing smuggled alchemical supplies marked with an unfamiliar sigil.
**Style:** Combat encounter woven into a social scene. The party must choose sides or protect everyone, then investigate the smuggled goods. Introduces the dock district, two guild leaders, and the city's political tensions within the first hour of play.
**Creatures:** 4 bandits (CR 1/8), 1 spy (CR 1) who tries to flee with evidence from the broken crate. Brokka and 2 dock workers fight alongside the party, engaging 2 of the bandits directly.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Party effectively faces 2 bandits + 1 spy. Base XP 250, multiplier x2 (3 monsters) = 500 adjusted. **Hard** for level 1 (threshold 300). Appropriate with NPC support.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Brokka pays the party 25 gp and offers free lodging at the Dockmasters' bunkhouse for as long as they're in the city.
- **Loot:** The spy carries a *Potion of Healing*, 15 gp in Calishite coin, and a coded message (DC 13 Intelligence check to decipher — it lists delivery schedules for the smuggled goods).
- **Hidden find:** The broken crate contains 6 vials of *Alchemist's Fire* among the smuggled supplies. A DC 14 Investigation check of the remaining cargo reveals a hidden compartment with a silver signet ring bearing the unfamiliar sigil — worth 50 gp to a collector, but far more valuable as evidence.
- **Side opportunity:** A dock worker mentions that a similar crate was delivered to a warehouse in the Tanners' Quarter last week. Investigating this lead (DC 12 Persuasion to get the address) gives the party a head start on Quest 3.
**Plot hook:** Brokka thanks the party and offers them work. The sigil on the smuggled goods belongs to no known guild — it's the first breadcrumb pointing to Sorn's hidden network. The Merchants' Guild clerk, genuinely confused, suggests someone forged the manifest — an early hint of doppelganger activity.

### Quest 2: The Funeral Procession (Level 2)

**Overview:** During Guildmaster Thera's public funeral, a gang of thugs attacks the procession, targeting Thera's grieving daughter Maren. The party must protect the mourners and pursue the attackers through the city streets. Brother Aldous is among the mourners, offering last rites — the party sees him briefly before the attack.
**Style:** Street chase and combat encounter split into two phases. Phase 1: 3 thugs attack the procession (the party fights them off). Phase 2: the bandit captain and his remaining thugs are cornered at the end of the chase. Maren becomes an ally and patron.
**Creatures:** Phase 1: 3 thugs (CR 1/2). Phase 2: 1 bandit captain (CR 2) with 1 thug who flees if the captain falls. Total across both phases: 4 thugs + 1 bandit captain.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Phase 1: 3 thugs = 300 base x2 = 600 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 600). Phase 2: 1 bandit captain + 1 thug = 550 base x1.5 = 825 adjusted. **Deadly** (threshold 800) but barely, and the party can pursue at their own pace. Splitting the encounter makes both phases survivable.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Maren offers the party 50 gp and her mother's *Cloak of Protection* (+1 AC and saving throws) — a family heirloom she wants used against whoever killed Thera.
- **Loot:** The bandit captain carries a *+1 dagger*, 30 gp in Calishite coin, and a sealed letter with instructions to "make an example of the Loomwright girl." The letter is unsigned but written on expensive parchment with a faint perfume — the same scent found at Thornwall Manor.
- **Hidden find:** If the party searches the rooftops along the chase route (DC 15 Perception), they find a spotter's nest with a spyglass (worth 100 gp) and a hand-drawn map of Maren's daily movements. Someone has been watching her for weeks.
- **Side opportunity:** The bandit captain, if captured and interrogated (DC 14 Intimidation), reveals his gang was camped in an abandoned mill outside the city walls. Raiding it yields 80 gp, a cask of Calishite wine (worth 25 gp), and a *Scroll of Detect Magic*.
**Plot hook:** The captured thugs claim they were hired anonymously but paid in rare Calishite coin — a detail that will recur. Maren confides she doesn't believe her mother's death was natural. If the party visits Brother Aldous at his temple afterwards, he quietly admits he sensed "something unnatural" about Thera's death — a faint trace of fiendish magic, though he cannot be certain.

### Quest 3: Trouble at the Tannery (Level 3)

**Overview:** Workers at the Tanners' Quarter are falling ill with a strange malady. Guildmaster Harsk Copperkettle personally asks the party to investigate — he's desperate and doesn't trust the city watch. The party discovers the water supply has been poisoned by alchemical runoff from a hidden laboratory beneath an abandoned building.
**Style:** Investigation and dungeon exploration. The lab is trapped and contains evidence of dark experiments. The doppelganger is encountered separately from the lab guardians — it reveals itself when the party returns to the surface, having observed their investigation.
**Creatures:** Lab encounter: 1 animated armor (CR 1), 2 alchemy-warped zombies (CR 1/4). Surface encounter: 1 doppelganger (CR 3) posing as a helpful local healer.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Lab: base 300, multiplier x2 = 600 adjusted. **Medium** (threshold 600). Doppelganger alone: 700, multiplier x1 = 700 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 900). Both encounters are appropriate for level 3 when separated.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Harsk Copperkettle pays the party 75 gp and gifts them a *set of masterwork artisan's tools* from the Tanners' Guild (worth 50 gp). He also offers the party a standing discount at any Tanners' Quarter shop (10% off).
- **Loot:** The lab contains 3 *Potions of Healing*, a *Herbalism Kit*, and 40 gp worth of alchemical ingredients. The animated armour, once defeated, yields a suit of *splint armour* in good condition. The doppelganger carries a *Ring of Mind Shielding* (which it uses to hide its thoughts from casual detection).
- **Hidden find:** A DC 16 Investigation check in the lab reveals a concealed floor safe containing 120 gp, a *Scroll of Lesser Restoration*, and a journal detailing poison recipes. The journal's handwriting matches notes found on the spy in Quest 1 — connecting the smuggling and poisoning operations.
- **Side opportunity:** The blackmail dossier names a wealthy merchant who is paying protection money. If the party visits this merchant (a nervous jeweller named Torvin), he gratefully offers them a *Pearl of Power* in exchange for destroying the copy of his file — or the party can leverage the information themselves.
**Plot hook:** The lab contains notes referencing "the master's preparations" and a list of city officials with notes on their habits and vulnerabilities — a blackmail dossier. The doppelganger, if unmasked, is one of Sorn's agents. Harsk Copperkettle is shaken but grateful — this is the first crack in his cautious neutrality.

## Act II: The Web Tightens (Levels 4-6)

Sorn is elected to the council. New laws restrict movement and commerce. Dissidents begin vanishing. The party transitions from hired muscle to active investigators working with Maren's underground resistance. These quests become available organically — the Guild Trials are a public event anyone can hear about, the Silent Ward is discovered through exploration or rumour, the Warehouse Wars erupt when Sorn's regulations bite, and the rest flow from the party's growing involvement with the resistance. The party may encounter these in any order and can skip optional quests entirely (see Quest Structure above for consequences).

### Quest 4: Guild Trials (Level 4)

**Overview:** To gain standing in Ashenmere, the party is invited to compete in the Guild Trials — a public contest of skill sponsored by the five houses. Events include a combat tourney, a riddle challenge, a rooftop race, and a negotiation contest. Vaelith Sorn presides as guest of honour and is publicly charming. All five guild leaders are present and can be spoken to.
**Style:** Social and skill challenge montage — minimal combat, heavy roleplay. Players meet key NPCs from each guild, including the Valiant Company (rival adventurers).
**Creatures:** The Valiant Company (friendly competitors who recur later). No monsters.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** The trial winners receive a *Guild Champion's Medallion* — a gold medallion worth 100 gp that grants advantage on Persuasion checks when dealing with guild members in Ashenmere. Each guild leader also offers a token gift: Brokka gives a *Potion of Water Breathing*, Harsk gives a masterwork leather belt (worth 25 gp), and Selene presents a *gold-inlaid compass* (worth 75 gp, functions as a mundane compass).
- **Loot:** The confiscated charm amulet can be studied with a DC 14 Arcana check, revealing it radiates enchantment magic of a type uncommon in mortal crafting. A DC 18 Arcana check identifies faint Infernal runes — this becomes critical evidence in Quest 9 if kept.
- **Hidden find:** A DC 15 Perception check during the rooftop race reveals a hidden alcove overlooking the course, where someone has been watching with a scrying focus. The focus is a small obsidian mirror worth 150 gp. If identified (DC 15 Arcana), it's a *one-way scrying mirror* — someone was watching the trials remotely.
- **Side opportunity:** The Valiant Company's wizard, Wick, nervously asks the party if they've noticed "anything strange" about the city's magic. He's detected background Infernal resonance but is too scared to investigate alone. If the party follows up, he shares his notes — these provide advantage on Arcana checks to detect Sorn's magic throughout Act II.
**Plot hook:** A competitor is caught cheating using enchantment magic. The enchantment traces back to a charm amulet — the same kind Sorn uses to control his agents, though this isn't yet apparent. Selene Auris (Goldsmiths) is seen congratulating Sorn warmly — they appear to be allies.

### Quest 5: The Silent Ward (Level 4)

**Overview:** An entire neighbourhood has gone quiet — residents won't leave their homes or speak to outsiders. The party discovers a night hag has been tormenting the ward in their dreams, extracting secrets that she sells to Sorn's network.
**Style:** Horror-tinged investigation. Players must figure out why people are terrified before they can confront the source. Includes a dream sequence encounter where the party enters the Ethereal Border.
**Creatures:** The shadow mastiffs (2) guard the approach to the hag's lair in the Ethereal Border and are encountered first as a separate fight. The night hag (CR 5) is encountered alone afterwards and strongly prefers to bargain rather than fight — she flees to the Ethereal Plane if reduced to half HP. She only fights to the death if cornered with no escape.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Shadow mastiffs (2): base 900, multiplier x1.5 = 1,350 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 1,500). Night hag alone (if fought): 1,800, multiplier x1 = 1,800. **Hard-Deadly** (threshold 2,000). Separated, both encounters are manageable. DM should not combine them.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** The grateful ward residents offer the party free shelter and supplies whenever needed. If the hag is killed, the ward elder gifts the party a family heirloom: *Boots of Elvenkind* (advantage on Stealth checks).
- **Loot:** The shadow mastiffs' lair contains the gnawed remains of previous victims — among the bones: 60 gp, a *Potion of Greater Healing*, and a *+1 shield* still strapped to a dead adventurer's arm.
- **Hidden find:** The hag's lair (in the Ethereal Border) contains a *Heartstone* — a lustrous black gem that can cure any disease when pressed against the flesh of the afflicted (one use). It can also be used to detect fiends within 30 feet, glowing faintly red in their presence. This is enormously useful for detecting doppelgangers in Quests 9 and 13, or for confirming Sorn's nature without needing Brother Aldous's testimony.
- **Bargain reward:** If the party bargains with the hag instead of fighting her, she offers a *Dream Whisper Vial* — a dark glass vial that, when opened before sleep, allows the user to send a short message to any creature they've met (as per *Sending*, once per week). Useful for contacting allies across the city during Act III and IV.
- **Side opportunity:** A DC 14 Investigation of the hag's potion shelves reveals a *Potion of Clairvoyance* and a recipe for *Oil of Etherealness* (requires rare ingredients worth 200 gp to brew, but a skilled alchemist can make it). The Oil of Etherealness becomes relevant if the party needs to track fiends who escape to the Ethereal Plane.
**Plot hook:** The hag can be bargained with — she'll betray Sorn's secrets if the party offers her something valuable. She reveals Sorn has "a face that isn't his own." See Consequence Chains for how this choice affects later quests.

### Quest 6: Warehouse Wars (Level 5)

**Overview:** Sorn's new trade regulations are squeezing the Dockmasters' Guild. Brokka asks the party to protect a contraband shipment — legal goods being moved outside Sorn's new tariff system. The rival Merchants' Guild sends enforcers to intercept. Lady Thornwall has quietly approved the enforcement action, though she is acting under Sorn's pressure.
**Style:** Faction conflict — morally grey. Both sides have legitimate grievances. Combat on the docks at night with environmental hazards (cranes, cargo, water). The party can attempt diplomacy, fight for one side, or find a creative third option (e.g., exposing that Sorn wrote the regulations specifically to provoke this conflict).
**Creatures:** Merchant Guild enforcers: 2 veterans (CR 3), 3 thugs (CR 1/2), 1 spy (CR 1) reporting to Sorn. If the party takes too long, a merrow (CR 2) surfaces from the harbour, drawn by the noise — this is an optional complication, not an additional enemy to fight alongside the enforcers.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Without merrow: base 1,850 (6 monsters), multiplier x2 = 3,700 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 3,000). Appropriate for a faction skirmish at level 5.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward (Dockmasters):** Brokka gives the party a *Cloak of the Manta Ray* from a smuggler's cache and 100 gp. She also offers the party use of a small river skiff whenever they need it — useful for the ship assault in Quest 10.
- **Quest reward (Merchants/peace):** Lady Thornwall sends a discreet thank-you gift: a *Periapt of Wound Closure* and 150 gp. She includes a note hinting she wishes to speak privately — an early path into Quest 8.
- **Loot:** The spy reporting to Sorn carries an *Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location* (explains how Sorn's agents move undetected), 45 gp, and a coded ledger linking Sorn's trade regulations to specific Merchants' Guild contracts. The veterans each carry 20 gp and a *Potion of Healing*.
- **Hidden find:** A DC 15 Investigation of the disputed warehouse reveals a false wall concealing a small Sorn-controlled armoury: 2 sets of *chain mail*, 6 *shortswords*, and a locked chest (DC 16 Thieves' Tools) containing 200 gp and a *Scroll of See Invisibility* — particularly useful against the invisible imps in Quest 8.
- **Side opportunity:** A dock worker named Pilar saw the whole fight and knows a fence who buys "no-questions-asked" goods. Pilar can connect the party to Ashenmere's black market, where they can buy *Potions of Invisibility* (150 gp each) and other rare supplies at inflated prices.
**Plot hook:** Forces the party to pick a side in the guild conflict, or broker an uneasy peace. Either way, they learn that Sorn is deliberately provoking guild infighting to consolidate power. See Consequence Chains for downstream effects.

### Quest 7: The Disappeared (Level 5)

**Overview:** Maren's resistance contacts are vanishing. Maren personally accompanies the party to track a missing informant through Ashenmere's undercity — a network of old foundations, collapsed basements, and forgotten crypts beneath the streets — to find a secret prison run by Sorn's loyalists.
**Style:** Dungeon crawl through urban undercity. Stealth-focused with optional combat. Freeing prisoners is the goal, not clearing rooms. Maren provides local knowledge but is not a fighter — protecting her adds tension.
**Creatures:** City watch corrupted by Sorn (guards x6, 1 knight commander), 1 intellect devourer (CR 2) hidden inside the prison warden (foreshadows Sorn's more alien allies), trapped corridors. The guards are encountered in small groups of 2-3, not all at once.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Largest likely encounter (3 guards + knight + intellect devourer): base 1,225 (5 monsters), multiplier x2 = 2,450 adjusted. **Medium-Hard** (threshold 3,000). Appropriate for a stealth dungeon where combat is optional.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** The rescued prisoners share what they know — intelligence on guard patrol routes through the undercity (grants advantage on Stealth checks in the undercity for the rest of the campaign). Maren gifts the party her mother's *Sending Stones* (pair) so they can always reach each other.
- **Loot:** The knight commander carries a *+1 longsword*, *chain mail*, 50 gp, and a writ of authority bearing Sorn's personal seal (useful as evidence, and can be used to bluff past corrupt city guards with a DC 13 Deception check). The intellect devourer's host warden has keys to every cell and a *Potion of Greater Healing*.
- **Hidden find:** A DC 16 Investigation of the prison warden's office reveals a hidden drawer containing a *Bag of Holding* stuffed with confiscated prisoner belongings: 180 gp in assorted coin, a *Wand of Magic Detection* (7 charges), personal letters, and a brass compass that always points toward the Council Citadel (it's actually attuned to one of the ward stones — foreshadows Quest 17).
- **Side opportunity:** One of the rescued prisoners, a halfling locksmith named Twig, offers to pick any lock in the city for the party as thanks. He can also teach a party member proficiency in Thieves' Tools if they spend a downtime day with him. Twig knows a secret entrance into the Citadel's servant quarters — useful in Quest 18.
**Plot hook:** The rescued prisoners include a former council aide who overheard Sorn speaking in Infernal. One prisoner has been mentally broken and mutters about "the tiger that walks like a man." Maren is shaken by the scale of Sorn's operation and vows to escalate the resistance.

### Quest 8: Masquerade at Thornwall Manor (Level 6)

**Overview:** The party infiltrates a lavish masquerade ball at Lady Thornwall's estate, where Sorn is courting her support. Their goal: steal correspondence from Sorn's private meeting room that proves he's blackmailing council members. Maren provides the party with invitations through a contact in the Weavers' Guild. Sorn himself is present.
**Style:** Heist/social infiltration. Disguises, deception checks, navigating guards and social encounters. Combat is a failure state — if detected, the party must escape the manor. Multiple approaches: charm the guards, pick locks, create a distraction, or find a servant's passage.
**Creatures:** Guards and nobles (non-combat), 2 invisible imps (CR 1) acting as Sorn's surveillance, 1 cambion (CR 5) bodyguard in a side room. Sorn himself (rakshasa) is present but does not fight — if confronted, he denies everything smoothly and has the party ejected.
**Adjusted difficulty (if combat triggered):** 2 imps + cambion: base 2,200 (3 monsters), multiplier x2 = 4,400 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 3,600). Appropriate as punishment for failed stealth.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** If the party successfully steals the correspondence, Maren rewards them with 200 gp from the resistance fund. Lady Thornwall, if turned into an ally, privately gifts the party an *Amulet of Protection from Evil and Good* — a family relic from her grandmother, a retired adventurer. "You'll need it more than I will."
- **Loot:** Sorn's private meeting room contains a locked desk (DC 18 Thieves' Tools). Inside: 300 gp in mixed coin, a *Scroll of Counterspell*, and an Infernal contract naming three city officials as Sorn's bound agents (proof that holds up before a magistrate). The cambion bodyguard, if fought, carries a *+1 scimitar* and a *Ruby of the War Mage*.
- **Hidden find:** A DC 17 Perception check in the servant's passage reveals a hollow wall panel. Behind it: Lady Thornwall's late husband's adventuring gear — a *Mithral Chain Shirt*, a *Quiver of Ehlonna*, and a journal describing an expedition to a temple of Tyr in the hills (contains a rough map that makes Quest 12 easier to find and navigate, granting advantage on Survival checks to reach the temple).
- **Side opportunity:** A servant named Calla approaches the party during the ball, whispering that she has been forced to serve the cambion and has seen "terrible things" in Sorn's private quarters at the Citadel. She can draw a partial floor plan of the Citadel's upper level — useful in Quest 18.
**Plot hook:** The stolen letters confirm Sorn controls three of the five council seats through blackmail and debt. They also reference "the shipment from Ul-Razzam" — a consignment of cursed items arriving by sea. Lady Thornwall, if approached privately, may confess her fear of Sorn — she can be turned into an ally if the party promises to destroy his blackmail material.

### Quest 9: The Poisoned Quill (Level 6)

**Overview:** Vara Inkwell, a sympathetic journalist at the Ashenmere Broadsheet, has been printing pamphlets exposing Sorn's corruption. Now she's been framed for murder using forged evidence and enchanted witnesses. The party must prove her innocence before her trial at dawn.
**Style:** Noir investigation against a ticking clock. Interrogating witnesses (some enchanted, some doppelgangers), searching crime scenes for planted evidence, breaking enchantment spells on false witnesses, deciphering coded messages, and presenting evidence to a magistrate. Heavy puzzle and deduction mechanics — the party must assemble a case, not just fight their way through.
**Creatures:** Investigation encounters (separate from each other): 2 doppelgangers (CR 3) planted as false witnesses — if the party bargained with the hag in Quest 5, they know to test for shapechangers. Separately, a hired assassin (CR 8) is sent to silence Vara if the party gets close to the truth — the assassin targets Vara (not the PCs), giving the party a chance to intervene. The assassin flees after 2 rounds if it fails to reach Vara.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Doppelgangers (encountered during investigation, not together): 700 each, **Easy** individually. Assassin (ambush on Vara): 3,900, multiplier x1 = 3,900 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 3,600). The assassin is dangerous but targets the NPC, not the party — the party's goal is to protect Vara and drive off the assassin, not to kill it. The DM should give the party a Perception check (DC 15) to spot the assassin before it strikes. These encounters must NOT happen simultaneously.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Vara, if saved, gives the party a *Hat of Disguise* she acquired "from a source" — invaluable for infiltration in later quests. She also pledges the Broadsheet's resources to the resistance, printing whatever the party needs distributed.
- **Loot:** The doppelgangers carry 30 gp each and *charm amulets* identical to the one found in Quest 4. Collecting three or more of these amulets provides enough evidence to convince even sceptical NPCs (like Harsk or Selene) of a coordinated enchantment conspiracy. The assassin, if defeated or driven off, drops a *+1 rapier* with an Infernal inscription ("silence the tongue") and a vial of *Assassin's Blood* poison (DC 10 Con save, 6/1d12 poison damage).
- **Hidden find:** Vara's cell at the courthouse has been scratched with a coded message (DC 13 Investigation to find, DC 14 Intelligence to decode). It's Vara's backup — names and locations of three more Sorn agents she hadn't yet published. This intelligence makes identifying the traitor in Quest 13 significantly easier (the party can cross-reference the names).
- **Side opportunity:** The presiding magistrate, if the party presents compelling evidence, privately admits he suspects Sorn but is too afraid to act openly. He offers to "lose" certain arrest warrants targeting the resistance — granting the party and their allies freedom of movement in the city for the rest of Act II.
**Plot hook:** See Consequence Chains for the journalist's fate and its downstream effects. If the party completed Quest 8 first, the stolen correspondence provides additional evidence for Vara's defence, making the trial easier to win.

## Act III: Unmasking the Fiend (Levels 7-8)

The party now knows the scope of the conspiracy but not Sorn's true nature. This act is about uncovering what he really is and finding the means to stop him, while Sorn moves to crush the resistance once and for all. The cursed cargo (Q10) can be discovered through multiple paths — Maren's spies, the masquerade letters, or Aldous's smuggled plea. The civil unrest (Q11) erupts when Sorn decides the party has become enough of a threat. The temple (Q12), traitor (Q13), and sermon (Q14) are all available once the party knows Sorn is a rakshasa, and can be pursued in any order.

### Quest 10: The Cursed Cargo (Level 7)

**Overview:** The party intercepts the ship carrying Sorn's consignment — a vessel crewed by charmed sailors carrying crates of cursed weapons and armour intended to equip Sorn's personal militia. The ship is also carrying a prisoner: Brother Aldous, the cleric of Tyr the party met briefly in Act I, who was captured after he discovered Sorn's secret.
**Style:** Ship-based assault. Board the vessel, fight through the crew, deal with the cursed cargo. Multiple approaches: sneak aboard at night, pose as harbour inspectors, or launch a direct assault from a hired boat.
**Creatures:** Charmed sailors (8 bandits, CR 1/8 — can be subdued non-lethally), 1 bearded devil (CR 3) guarding the hold, 2 spined devils (CR 2) on the rigging, 1 animated armour (CR 1) from the cursed cargo activating mid-fight. The bandits are individually trivial and inflate the monster count — the real threats are the three devils.
**Adjusted difficulty:** All creatures: base 2,000 (12 monsters), multiplier x3 = 6,000 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 4,400 Hard / 6,800 Deadly). On paper this looks steep, but the bandits barely threaten level 7 PCs and drop in one hit. The effective fight is 3 devils + animated armour = 2,050 base, x2 = 4,100. **Medium-Hard** in practice. Appropriate.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Brother Aldous bestows a *Blessing of Tyr* on the party — each member gains 10 temporary HP that refreshes after each long rest for the remainder of Act III. He also gives the party his personal *Holy Symbol of Tyr* (silver, worth 50 gp), which functions as a spellcasting focus and grants advantage on Religion checks related to fiends.
- **Loot:** The bearded devil carries a *glaive of infernal make* — a mundane glaive that glows faintly red and deals an extra 1d4 fire damage (effectively a *+0 weapon with a minor enchantment*). The spined devils each drop 2 *spined devil spines* (can be thrown as improvised weapons, 1d4+1 piercing, range 20/60). The ship's cargo hold contains 500 gp worth of cursed weapons and armour — worthless in their current state, but a cleric or paladin can purify one weapon per long rest (5th-level spell slot), creating a temporary blessed weapon (see Slaying a Rakshasa).
- **Hidden find:** The captain's cabin contains a locked sea chest (DC 16 Thieves' Tools). Inside: a *Cloak of Elvenkind*, 250 gp in Calishite trade bars, a nautical chart showing a route from Ul-Razzam (a city in the Nine Hells — or its Material Plane proxy), and a manifest listing two more shipments due to arrive. Intercepting these future shipments (a side activity the party can pursue during downtime) yields additional cursed weapons to purify and 300 gp per shipment.
- **Side opportunity:** The charmed sailors, once freed from enchantment, include a first mate named Kaspar who knows the sea routes Sorn's agents use. Kaspar can be recruited to blockade the harbour during the siege (Quest 18), preventing fiendish reinforcements from arriving by sea.
**Plot hook:** Brother Aldous, now rescued, confirms Sorn is a fiend — a rakshasa. He explains they are immune to spells of 6th level or lower, resistant to mundane weapons, and can only be permanently killed with a blessed piercing weapon. He knows where one might be found — a ruined temple of Tyr in the hills. The cursed weapons themselves can be purified as an alternative (see Slaying a Rakshasa).

### Quest 11: Civil Unrest (Level 7)

**Overview:** Sorn declares martial law after a "terrorist attack" staged by his own agents. The city guard, now loyal to him, clashes with guild militias in the streets. The party must navigate a city in chaos — protecting civilians, rallying resistance fighters, and preventing Sorn's forces from seizing the last independent guild hall. Maren is on the front lines coordinating the resistance.
**Style:** Large-scale urban combat across multiple street encounters. The party makes tactical decisions about where to deploy — they can't be everywhere at once. Choices have consequences: saving the guild hall might mean the market district is overrun, or vice versa. If Brother Aldous was rescued in Quest 10, his followers defend the temple district and provide a safe haven.
**Creatures:** The party faces ONE of the following major encounters (their choice of where to deploy), while resistance NPCs handle the other:
- **Guild Hall Defence:** 1 barbed devil (CR 5), 1 veteran (CR 3), 1 knight (CR 3). Resistance fighters engage the knight, leaving the party to face the barbed devil + veteran. Effective encounter: base 2,500 (2 monsters), multiplier x1.5 = 3,750 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 4,400).
- **Market District:** 1 gladiator (CR 5), 3 berserkers (CR 2). Base 3,150 (4 monsters), multiplier x2 = 6,300 adjusted. **Hard-Deadly** (threshold 6,800). The berserkers fight recklessly but have low AC (13), making them glass cannons the party can drop quickly.
**Additional complications:** Hostile civilians if the journalist was not saved in Quest 9 (hazard, not enemies — they block movement and throw debris, imposing disadvantage on attacks in certain areas).
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Whichever location the party defends successfully, the grateful defenders provide material support: the Guild Hall yields 300 gp from the guild treasury and a *Ring of Resistance (fire)* from Brokka's personal vault; the Market District yields 200 gp in looted coin, salvaged goods worth 150 gp, and a *Potion of Heroism* from a grateful apothecary.
- **Loot:** The barbed devil drops a *Flame Tongue longsword* — a rare weapon that deals an extra 2d6 fire damage while ignited (command word "burn"). The gladiator (if fought instead) carries a *+1 shield* emblazoned with a devil's face and a *Belt of Hill Giant Strength* (Str 21) — a significant power boost for a martial character heading into Act III.
- **Hidden find:** In the aftermath, a DC 14 Investigation of the battlefield reveals a dead Sorn agent carrying a *sending stone* (paired with one in the Citadel). The party can use this to eavesdrop on Sorn's communications — once per day, a DC 16 Arcana check allows them to listen in on the last message sent through the paired stone. This grants intelligence advantages for the rest of the campaign.
- **Side opportunity:** A wounded city guard named Darvon, disillusioned by seeing a devil fighting alongside his colleagues, defects to the resistance. He knows the watch rotation schedules and has a master key to the city's gate towers — both useful during the siege (Quest 18).
**Plot hook:** During the battle, Sorn's mask slips — literally. A resistance fighter wounds him and his human disguise briefly flickers, revealing tiger-like features. The secret is out, but half the city is too afraid or charmed to care. The party must now rally enough support for a final push.

### Quest 12: The Old Temple (Level 8)

**Overview:** Brother Aldous directs the party to a ruined temple of Tyr in the hills outside Ashenmere, where a blessed weapon rests in the crypt of a long-dead paladin. The temple has been corrupted by fiendish influence and is now home to devils sent by Sorn to ensure no one recovers the weapon.
**Style:** Classic temple dungeon — puzzle rooms, divine trials (tests of justice, mercy, and courage), and combat. The paladin's spirit appears and tests the party's worthiness. The trials can be overcome through wisdom, compassion, or courage — not just combat prowess.
**Creatures:** The dungeon has three main encounters:
- **Upper Temple (guardian pair):** 1 barbed devil (CR 5), 1 chain devil (CR 8). Base 5,700, multiplier x1.5 = 8,550 adjusted. **Deadly** (threshold 8,400) but barely. This is the hardest fight in the dungeon and should be telegraphed — the party can hear the chain devil's rattling from a distance and prepare.
- **Lower Crypt (atmospheric):** 8-12 lemures (CR 0). Trivial in XP but atmospheric — they reform after being destroyed, making the encounter about finding a way through (holy water, Turn Undead, consecrated ground) rather than killing them all.
- **Crypt Vault (boss):** 1 bone devil (CR 9) with 2 bearded devil (CR 3) guards, plus lair actions (spectral chains erupt from the floor on initiative 20 — DC 14 Dex save or restrained; necrotic cold aura deals 2d6 cold damage to creatures starting their turn within 10 ft of the vault door). Base 6,400 (3 monsters), multiplier x2 = 12,800 adjusted. **Deadly** (threshold 8,400). Appropriate for a dungeon boss — the bone devil is the real threat while the bearded devils add action economy pressure.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** The **Blade of Tyr's Witness** — a *+2 shortsword* that deals an extra 2d6 radiant damage against fiends and can permanently slay a rakshasa. It glows with golden light when fiends are within 60 feet. The paladin's spirit also bestows a *Blessing of the Martyr* on the party: once during the campaign, a downed party member can choose to rise with 1 HP and immediately take a turn (a legendary act of will).
- **Loot:** The bone devil's hoard includes 600 gp in ancient dwarven coin, a *Necklace of Prayer Beads* (with 2 beads: Blessing and Curing), and a suit of *+1 half plate* etched with prayers to Tyr. The barbed devil (upper temple) drops a *barbed whip* — a mundane weapon that deals 1d4+1 piercing and grapples on a hit (DC 14 to escape). The chain devil drops lengths of enchanted chain that can be fashioned into a *Chain Net* (as a net, but DC 15 to escape and cannot be easily cut).
- **Hidden find:** A DC 17 Investigation of the paladin's tomb reveals a hidden reliquary beneath the sarcophagus. Inside: a *Periapt of Proof Against Poison*, a vial of *Holy Water* (consecrated by Tyr himself — deals 4d6 radiant to fiends instead of the normal 2d6), and the paladin's journal describing how he killed a rakshasa centuries ago. The journal details the creature's fighting style and weaknesses — granting the reader advantage on the first 3 attack rolls against Sorn in the final battle.
- **Side opportunity:** The temple's lower level contains a sealed meditation chamber. A DC 15 Religion check to pray before the shrine of Tyr grants a divine vision showing the locations of Sorn's three ward stones (Quest 17) — if the party hasn't already discovered them through other means.
**Plot hook:** The party recovers the **Blade of Tyr's Witness** — a blessed shortsword that can permanently slay a rakshasa. The paladin's spirit warns that Sorn has died and returned before; this time must be final. If the party completed Quest 13 first, the traitor's intelligence reveals which temple chambers are trapped, allowing the party to avoid the upper temple encounter entirely.

### Quest 13: The Traitor Unmasked (Level 8)

**Overview:** A trusted member of Maren's resistance is secretly a doppelganger who has been feeding information to Sorn. After a resistance safehouse is raided and allies are captured, the party must identify the traitor from among their closest NPCs before Sorn dismantles the resistance entirely.
**Style:** Social deduction and paranoia. The party must investigate their own allies — comparing stories, testing for shapechangers (easier if they learned about doppelgangers in earlier quests), setting traps with false information, and watching for inconsistencies. Ends with a confrontation when the traitor tries to assassinate Maren.
**Creatures:** The greater doppelganger is a custom stat block (CR 5, AC 16, 90 HP, multiattack 2 slams for 2d6+4 each, Surprise Attack deals 3d6 extra, Read Thoughts at will, immune to charmed condition). It fights alone when exposed — the 2 invisible imps (CR 1) flee to warn Sorn rather than fight. If the doppelganger fails, a hired assassin (CR 8) arrives 2 rounds later as backup — this gives the party a chance to prepare rather than being surprised.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Greater doppelganger alone: 1,800, multiplier x1 = 1,800 adjusted. **Easy-Medium** (threshold 3,600). With assassin arriving later (separate phase): 3,900, multiplier x1 = 3,900 adjusted. **Medium-Hard**. The DM should NOT run both simultaneously. The doppelganger is unmasked and dealt with; then the assassin attacks. Total encounter resources are taxing but split across two phases.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Maren rewards the party with 200 gp and grants them full access to the resistance's armoury: 4 *Potions of Greater Healing*, a *Scroll of Dispel Magic*, and a *Scroll of Remove Curse*. The resistance's trust in the party is now absolute.
- **Loot:** The greater doppelganger carries a *Circlet of Blasting* (3 charges, *Scorching Ray*) and 80 gp. The assassin (if defeated) drops a *Cloak of Displacement* — an extremely powerful defensive item that imposes disadvantage on attack rolls against the wearer.
- **Hidden find:** The doppelganger's personal effects include a journal written in a cramped, alien hand. A DC 16 Intelligence check reveals it's written in Deep Speech. If translated (by a linguist, magic, or *Comprehend Languages*), it contains detailed notes on the resistance's operations — but also the doppelganger's private observations about Sorn. It notes that Sorn "grows careless in his vanity" and that "the portal chamber's anchors are weakest at the cardinal points." This intelligence directly benefits Quest 20, allowing the party to identify the anchor points without trial and error.
- **Side opportunity:** If the assassin escapes, they drop a *Hellhound Cloak* — a magical cloak that allows the wearer to cast *Hellish Rebuke* once per long rest (3d10 fire damage) and grants resistance to fire damage. The party can track the fleeing assassin to a hidden cache (DC 15 Survival): 400 gp, a *Potion of Invisibility*, and a poisoner's kit.
**Plot hook:** The traitor, if captured alive, reveals the layout of Sorn's defences in the Council Citadel and confirms the fiend is preparing a summoning ritual to bring more of his kind through from the Nine Hells — specifically, enough to return to Mahavan's court with a show of force. This intelligence makes the Citadel assault (Act IV) easier.

### Quest 14: The Last Sermon (Level 8)

**Overview:** Brother Aldous plans to deliver a public sermon in the Old Quarter, denouncing Sorn as a fiend before a crowd of frightened citizens. This is a bold move — if successful, it shatters Sorn's carefully maintained image and rallies divine support. But Sorn sends agents to silence Aldous before he can speak.
**Style:** Defence encounter with social elements. The party must protect Brother Aldous while he delivers his sermon (which takes 10 rounds). Waves of enemies attack from different directions while the crowd panics. Between waves, Aldous's words move the crowd — if the party can keep him alive, civilians begin helping (barricading streets, warning of approaching enemies). If Aldous completes the sermon, he performs a consecration ritual on a second weapon, creating a backup blessed weapon.
**Creatures:** Wave 1 (round 1): 4 thugs (CR 1/2) rush the stage. Wave 2 (round 4): 2 veterans (CR 3) attack from the rooftops. Wave 3 (round 7): 1 cambion (CR 5) arrives to kill Aldous personally.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Wave 1: base 400, multiplier x2 = 800 adjusted. **Trivial** — these are meant to be dispatched quickly. Wave 2: base 1,400, multiplier x1.5 = 2,100 adjusted. **Easy-Medium** — rooftop positioning makes them harder to reach. Wave 3: base 1,800, multiplier x1 = 1,800 adjusted. **Easy-Medium** — but the party may be spread out from handling earlier waves. Cumulative across all waves (no rest): base 3,600 total. Manageable but taxing. The real challenge is positional — protecting Aldous while enemies attack from multiple angles.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** If the sermon succeeds, Brother Aldous consecrates a backup blessed weapon — the party chooses which weapon (any piercing weapon they own). It gains the *blessed* property permanently and deals an extra 1d6 radiant damage to fiends. The reinvigorated Church of Tyr also provides 4 *Potions of Greater Healing* and a *Scroll of Greater Restoration*.
- **Loot:** The cambion drops a *+1 longsword* wreathed in shadow, 100 gp, and Sorn's written kill order for Aldous — proof of Sorn's direct involvement in violence against the clergy. The veterans each carry 25 gp and a *Potion of Healing*.
- **Hidden find:** If the party searches the area after the battle (DC 14 Investigation), they find that one of the thugs was carrying a *stone of far speech* — a small polished stone that, when activated, transmits everything said within 30 feet to a paired stone (currently held by Sorn). If the party realises what it is (DC 15 Arcana), they can use it to feed Sorn false information throughout Act IV, granting advantage on Deception checks to mislead his forces.
- **Side opportunity:** After the sermon, a retired dwarven smith named Grundar approaches the party. He's inspired by Aldous's words and offers to reforge any weapon the party owns into a *+1 weapon* for free (materials included) — the work takes one day. If they bring him the cursed weapons from Quest 10, he can purify and reforge one into a *+2 weapon* (two days of work).
**Plot hook:** If Aldous completes the sermon, the Church of Tyr is reinvigorated — faithful citizens openly defy Sorn's curfews. If Aldous is killed or the sermon is disrupted, the Church remains broken and the party loses a key ally for the finale. Either way, Sorn is furious — this accelerates his plans and triggers the events of Act IV.

## Act IV: The Gilded Throne (Levels 9-11)

Sorn retreats to the Council Citadel and fortifies it with fiendish allies. The city is divided. The party must rally their forces, break through his defences, and kill the rakshasa with the blessed weapon before he completes a ritual to open a permanent gate to the Nine Hells.

Once the party commits to confronting Sorn directly, they need allies (Q15). Sorn strikes back (Q16) in response to their preparations. The ward stones (Q17) are discovered through intelligence gathered from Q13 or Q16. From the sabotage onward (Q17-Q22), events cascade — the siege launches at dawn after the wards fall, and the finale plays out as a continuous operation through the Citadel.

### Quest 15: The Outlander Alliance (Level 9)

**Overview:** Ashenmere alone cannot defeat Sorn's forces. The party travels outside the city to forge alliances. The available allies depend partly on the party's earlier choices and relationships — guild leaders they've cultivated may offer forces, while alienated factions must be won over with harder tasks.
**Style:** Diplomacy-focused with wilderness travel encounters. Each potential ally has a price or a task.
**Potential allies:**
- **Brokka's Dockworkers** — Available if the party has maintained good relations with the Dockmasters. They provide a militia for the siege.
- **Lady Thornwall's Household Guard** — Available if the party freed her from Sorn's blackmail in Quest 8. She provides soldiers and intelligence about the Citadel's layout.
- **The Ironholt Dwarves** — A sceptical dwarven hold in the hills. They want proof of the fiendish threat (the cursed weapons or Brother Aldous's testimony). They provide heavy infantry.
- **The Thornwood Druids** — A druid circle whose forest Sorn's loggers have been clear-cutting. They want a sacred grove protected from a young green dragon that Sorn has goaded into the forest. They provide nature magic support.
- **The Valiant Company** — The rival adventurers from the Guild Trials. They want a share of the glory. They fight alongside the party during the siege.
- **Selene Auris's Goldsmiths** — Only available if the party has provided undeniable proof of Sorn's fiendish nature. The hardest faction to turn, but their wealth funds the entire operation.
**Creatures:** Travel encounters: 1 troll (CR 5) ambush in the hills — a straightforward Medium fight for level 9. 3 hell hounds (CR 3) sent by Sorn to intercept — base 2,100, x2 = 4,200 adjusted, **Medium**. The druid quest involves confronting a young green dragon (CR 8) — base 3,900, x1 = 3,900 adjusted, **Medium**. All travel encounters are appropriately easy-to-medium for level 9.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Each allied faction provides a tangible benefit:
  - *Brokka's Dockworkers:* A *Folding Boat* and 150 gp in dockside wages.
  - *Lady Thornwall's Guard:* Citadel blueprints (advantage on Navigation checks inside the Citadel) and 200 gp.
  - *The Ironholt Dwarves:* A set of *Adamantine Armour* (medium, negates critical hits) and a barrel of *dwarven firebombs* (6 flasks, as *Alchemist's Fire* but 2d6 damage).
  - *The Thornwood Druids:* A *Staff of the Woodlands* and 3 *Potions of Vitality*.
  - *The Valiant Company:* A *Bag of Tricks (grey)* and Wick's personal *Wand of Fireballs* (3 charges remaining — "I'll make another").
  - *Selene Auris's Goldsmiths:* 1,000 gp in funding and a *Ring of Spell Storing* containing one casting of *Haste*.
- **Loot:** The troll carries 80 gp in a crude sack and a *Javelin of Lightning*. The hell hounds drop *Hellhound Teeth* (6 total, can be crafted into arrowheads that deal +1d4 fire damage per arrow — a skilled smith needs 1 hour per 3 arrows). The young green dragon's hoard contains 800 gp, 1,200 sp, a *set of emerald earrings* (worth 250 gp), a *Scroll of Protection from Energy*, and a *Wand of Web* (7 charges).
- **Hidden find:** The druids' sacred grove, once the dragon is cleared, contains an ancient tree with *Goodberries* growing naturally (the party can harvest 2d10 goodberries, each restoring 1 HP, that never spoil). A DC 16 Nature check reveals a natural spring in the grove — drinking from it removes one level of exhaustion and cures one disease.
**Plot hook:** With allies secured, the resistance has enough strength to challenge Sorn's hold on the city. The party returns to find Ashenmere under a magical curfew — fiendish energy crackles in the sky above the Citadel. The number and strength of allies affects the siege's difficulty.

### Quest 16: Sorn's Retribution (Level 9)

**Overview:** Sorn strikes back. Having learned of the party's alliance-building, he sends a coordinated strike team to destroy the resistance's headquarters and assassinate Maren. The party must defend the safehouse against a surprise night assault while protecting key NPCs.
**Style:** Defensive siege encounter. The party chooses how to fortify the safehouse before the attack (barricading doors, setting traps, positioning allies). The assault comes in three waves with brief respite between each. Allied NPCs (resistance fighters) hold secondary positions but can be overwhelmed if the party doesn't support them.
**Creatures:** Wave 1: 4 veterans (CR 3) breach the front entrance. Base 2,800, multiplier x2 = 5,600 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 6,400). Wave 2: 2 barbed devils (CR 5) attack from the roof. Base 3,600, multiplier x1.5 = 5,400 adjusted. **Hard**. Wave 3: 1 assassin (CR 8) infiltrates during the chaos, targeting Maren specifically. Base 3,900, x1 = 3,900 adjusted. **Medium** — but the party may be weakened from prior waves. The assassin is the climactic threat.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Cumulative across all waves without a long rest, this is a serious resource drain. Each wave individually is Hard or below, but the total (base 10,300 across 3 waves) taxes HP and spell slots heavily. Short rest between waves 2 and 3 is possible if the party is quick.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Successfully defending the safehouse earns the resistance's undying loyalty. Maren provides the party with a *Helm of Telepathy* recovered from a defeated Sorn agent — it allows *Detect Thoughts* at will and *Suggestion* once per day. "Know your enemy's mind before they know yours."
- **Loot:** The veterans each carry 30 gp and a *Potion of Healing*. The barbed devils drop *infernal barbs* (10 total, can be used as caltrops that deal 2d4 piercing + 1d4 fire damage). The assassin carries a *Dagger of Venom* (once per day, coat the blade — DC 15 Con save or 2d10 poison + poisoned for 1 minute), 150 gp, and a map of the resistance's known safehouses with Sorn's annotations ("priority targets" circled in red ink).
- **Hidden find:** Among the captured veterans' gear, a DC 15 Investigation check reveals one is carrying a *Ward Stone Shard* — a fragment that pulses with the same energy as the Citadel's protective barrier. A DC 16 Arcana check confirms it resonates with the full ward stones, pointing toward them like a compass. This makes locating the ward stones in Quest 17 significantly easier.
- **Side opportunity:** The resistance fighters who helped defend the safehouse include a former Citadel guard named Brenn. If the party speaks with her, she reveals a flaw in the Citadel's sewer grating — one section is rusted and can be breached silently. This opens the sewer route in Quest 18 without needing to have completed Quest 7.
**Plot hook:** The assault demonstrates Sorn's desperation — he's accelerating his timeline. The captured veterans reveal the Citadel's ward stones (setting up Quest 17). Maren, whether saved or narrowly rescued, steels herself for the final push.

### Quest 17: Sabotage (Level 9)

**Overview:** The Council Citadel is protected by three ward stones hidden across the city, each guarded by one of Sorn's lieutenants. The party must destroy all three in a single night before Sorn can reinforce them, clearing the way for the assault.
**Style:** Three mini-encounters back to back, each with a different approach. Ticking clock — the party must complete all three before dawn.
**The three ward stones:**
- **The Flooded Sewer** — Brute-force combat. A hezrou demon (CR 8) guards the ward stone in a half-submerged chamber. Environmental hazards: waist-deep water (difficult terrain), toxic fumes (DC 12 Con save each round or poisoned). Base 3,900, x1 = 3,900 adjusted. **Medium**. Appropriate for the opening encounter of the night.
- **The Bell Tower** — Stealth and traps. The tower is rigged with alarm glyphs and mechanical traps. 2 chain devils (CR 8) patrol but can be avoided entirely with careful stealth. The ward stone is at the top. Triggering an alarm brings reinforcements the party cannot fight — stealth is essential. If the party fights the chain devils: base 7,800, x1.5 = 11,700 adjusted. **Deadly** — this is intentional. The encounter is designed to be avoided, not fought. The DM should make this clear through environmental storytelling (bones of previous intruders, the sound of rattling chains echoing through the tower).
- **The Grand Theatre** — Social deception. A succubus (CR 4) controls a crowd of charmed nobles attending a late-night performance. The ward stone is hidden on stage. The party must extract it without breaking the succubus's hold on the crowd (which would cause a panic) or being identified. The succubus is trivial in combat (base 1,100) but the real challenge is social — extracting the stone without collateral damage.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Each ward stone, when shattered, releases a burst of contained energy that can be captured in a suitable vessel (an empty vial, a gemstone, etc.). These *Infernal Energy Shards* (3 total) can each be used once to cast *Dispel Magic* at 5th level as an action — extremely useful against Sorn's enchantments in the finale.
- **Loot:** The hezrou drops a *Demon Ichor Vial* (can be applied to a weapon as an action, dealing +2d6 acid damage on the next hit) and 100 gp worth of corroded coin. The chain devils (if fought, which they shouldn't be) each drop 60 gp and a length of *Devil's Chain* (can be used as a 15-foot chain weapon, 1d8 bludgeoning, reach). The succubus drops a *Circlet of Charm* (3 charges, cast *Charm Person* at 3rd level, recharges 1d3 charges at dawn) and a *jewelled mask* worth 200 gp.
- **Hidden find:** The Bell Tower, if the party reaches the top without triggering alarms, has a panoramic view of the Citadel. A DC 14 Perception check from this vantage point reveals the Citadel's weak points — where the walls are thinnest, where guards are fewest, and where the fiendish energy is strongest (the ritual chamber entrance). This grants advantage on the party's first tactical decision in Quest 18.
- **Side opportunity:** The charmed nobles at the Grand Theatre, once freed from the succubus's influence, include a wealthy patron named Lord Belin who is horrified by what he's been doing under enchantment. He opens his personal armoury to the party: a *+1 longbow*, a *Shield of Missile Attraction* (cursed — the DM should let the party identify the curse with *Identify* before taking it), and 500 gp. He also pledges his household guard (12 soldiers) to the siege effort.
**Plot hook:** With the wards down, the Citadel's magical defences fall. The resistance launches its assault at dawn. The party takes a long rest overnight before the siege.

### Quest 18: The Siege of the Citadel (Level 10)

**Overview:** The resistance assaults the Council Citadel while the party leads a strike team inside. They must fight through Sorn's elite guard and fiendish allies, navigating the fortified halls toward the inner sanctum.
**Style:** Epic dungeon assault with multiple paths. The party chooses their approach:
- **The Front Gate** — Direct combat through the main halls. The hardest fight but the fastest route. Allied NPCs from Quest 15 engage the bulk of the guards, leaving the party to face a reduced force.
- **The Servant's Passage** — A stealth route through kitchens and back corridors. Fewer enemies but tight spaces and traps.
- **The Sewers Below** — Connects to the undercity from Quest 7. The party knows this route. Fewer guards but environmental hazards and a risk of getting lost.
**Creatures:** The encounters depend on the route chosen:
- **Front gate (with ally support):** 2 knights (CR 3), 1 champion (CR 9). Allies engage the other 2 knights. Base 6,400 (3 monsters), multiplier x2 = 12,800 adjusted. **Deadly** (threshold 11,200) but barely — appropriate for the dramatic front-gate assault. Without ally support (party alienated factions): 4 knights + champion = 7,800, x2 = 15,600. **Far Deadly** — punishes parties that failed to build alliances.
- **Servant's passage:** 1 barbed devil (CR 5) + traps. Effective base 1,800, x1 = 1,800. **Easy** — but the traps add challenge.
- **Sewer route:** 1 chain devil (CR 8) + environmental hazards. Base 3,900, x1 = 3,900. **Medium**.
- **Mid-boss (all routes converge):** Sorn's cambion (CR 5) lieutenant from the masquerade, now accompanied by 2 bearded devil (CR 3) bodyguards. Base 3,200 (3 monsters), multiplier x2 = 6,400 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 7,600). The cambion fights with tactical intelligence, using the bearded devils as a screen while attacking with fiery rays.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** The party finds a *War Room* just before the inner sanctum, where Sorn's battle plans are laid out. These include a complete roster of his remaining forces and the ritual's timeline — the party learns they have exactly 1 hour before the portal becomes permanent. The room also contains a locked cabinet (DC 17 Thieves' Tools) with 4 *Potions of Superior Healing*, 2 *Potions of Fire Resistance*, and a *Scroll of Globe of Invulnerability* (6th level — immune to spells of 5th level and below within the globe for 1 minute, enormously powerful against the rakshasa spawn in Quest 21).
- **Loot:** The cambion mid-boss carries a *Flame Tongue scimitar* (if the party didn't already get one in Quest 11), 200 gp, and Sorn's personal seal ring — a gold ring worth 300 gp that can be used to command Sorn's charmed guards to stand down (DC 12 Deception check, with advantage if the party has the cambion's body or the seal ring in hand). The champion (if fought at the front gate) wears *+1 plate armour* and carries a *Greatsword of Sharpness* (on a natural 20, the target takes an extra 4d6 slashing damage).
- **Hidden find:** The servant's passage route passes through Sorn's private quarters. A DC 18 Investigation check reveals a hidden vault containing Sorn's personal hoard: 2,000 gp in gemstones, a *Manual of Bodily Health* (increases Con by 2, to a max of 22 — can be read during a long rest), and a *Portable Hole*. There is also a portrait of a regal, tiger-headed figure labelled "Mahavan" — Sorn's former master. The painting radiates faint divination magic; destroying it severs Sorn's last connection to the Nine Hells hierarchy, making his summoned devils 1 round slower to arrive in the final battle.
**Plot hook:** The party reaches the inner sanctum. The ritual is active — fiendish light pours from below and the air tastes of brimstone.

### Quest 19: The Devil's Vanguard (Level 10)

**Overview:** As the party breaches the inner Citadel, the destabilising portal sends a pulse of fiendish energy through the building. A horned devil — far more powerful than anything the party has faced — tears through the Citadel walls and rampages through the resistance lines in the courtyard. If it isn't stopped, it will destroy the party's allied forces and cut off their retreat.
**Style:** Mini-boss fight in a collapsing environment. The courtyard is strewn with rubble, burning siege equipment, and fleeing soldiers. The horned devil is a straightforward combat encounter — no puzzles, no social elements, just a powerful fiend that needs to be put down before it massacres the resistance.
**Creatures:** 1 horned devil (CR 11, 178 HP, AC 18). Base 7,200, multiplier x1 = 7,200 adjusted. **Hard** (threshold 7,600). The horned devil has multiattack (fork + tail), Hurl Flame, and flight. It fights aggressively, targeting clusters of allied NPCs unless the party draws its attention. Appropriate as a Hard single-monster fight for level 10 — dangerous but not Deadly.
**Rewards:**
- **Loot:** The horned devil drops its *infernal fork* — a *+2 trident* that deals an extra 1d6 fire damage on a hit. It also drops a *Devil's Eye Gem* (a smoky red gemstone worth 500 gp that allows the holder to cast *See Invisibility* once per long rest). The devil's body dissolves into sulphur and ash, leaving behind a fist-sized chunk of *Infernal Iron* — a rare crafting material that a skilled smith could forge into armour or a weapon with fire resistance properties (requires a full day and a forge, so not usable until after the campaign, but valuable as a future hook).
- **Hidden find:** In the rubble where the horned devil breached the wall, a DC 15 Perception check reveals it smashed through an old sealed chamber — a forgotten armoury from the Citadel's original construction. Inside: a dusty *Shield of the Faithful* (+2 shield, once per day the wielder can cast *Shield of Faith* on themselves as a bonus action) and 6 *silvered crossbow bolts* in a rotting quiver.
**Plot hook:** Defeating the horned devil proves the portal is growing unstable. The party must descend to the ritual chamber immediately before it tears open completely. The resistance cheers — morale is restored — but the clock is ticking.

### Quest 20: The Ritual Chamber (Level 10)

**Overview:** Below the throne room, the summoning ritual blazes in a vast underground chamber. A portal to the Nine Hells is half-open, and devils are already pouring through. The party must disrupt the ritual before the gate becomes permanent.
**Style:** Combat and puzzle hybrid. Four anchor points sustain the ritual, and each must be disrupted differently:
- **Anchor 1** — A pillar of chains that must be physically broken (DC 20 Strength check or 50 damage to the pillar).
- **Anchor 2** — An infernal inscription that must be read backwards (DC 16 Intelligence/Arcana check, or a clue found in Brother Aldous's texts).
- **Anchor 3** — A sacrificial flame fed by trapped souls. The souls must be freed with divine magic (any spell with the "radiant" damage type or a DC 16 Religion check to pray them free).
- **Anchor 4** — A mirror reflecting the Nine Hells. Shattering it triggers a psychic backlash (DC 15 Wisdom save or 4d6 psychic damage) but breaks the anchor.
While the party works on the anchors, waves of devils attack and allied NPCs hold the flanks.
**Creatures:** Core defenders: 2 barbed devils (CR 5) at anchor points + 1 erinyes (CR 12) commanding the defence. Waves of lemures and bearded devils are handled narratively by allied NPCs — the party only directly fights what's at the anchors.
**Adjusted difficulty:** The party engages: 1 erinyes + 2 barbed devils. Base 12,000 (3 monsters), multiplier x2 = 24,000 adjusted. **Deadly x2** on paper. However, the erinyes is the commander and engages the party only after 2 anchors are disrupted (the party fights 1 barbed devil at a time initially). Effective encounters: Anchor fights (1 barbed devil each) = 1,800 each, **Easy**. Erinyes joins after half the anchors fall, creating a climactic Hard-Deadly moment. The DM should pace the erinyes's entry to create dramatic escalation, not front-load the danger.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** Each destroyed anchor releases a *Shard of Divine Light* — crystallised radiant energy that can be thrown as a ranged weapon attack (range 30/60, 3d8 radiant damage to fiends, 1d8 to all others, single use each). The party can collect up to 4 shards — powerful ammunition for the final battle.
- **Loot:** The erinyes drops a *+3 longbow* and a quiver of 10 *+1 arrows*. She also wears *winged armour* — a breastplate with retractable angel wings that grants a 30-foot fly speed for 1 hour per day (recharges at dawn). The barbed devils each drop 50 gp and a *Potion of Greater Healing*.
- **Hidden find:** The ritual chamber itself contains Sorn's research notes on the portal mechanics. A DC 17 Arcana check reveals that the portal can be reversed — instead of opening to the Nine Hells, it can be turned into a *banishment conduit* for 1 round. If the party uses this knowledge during the final battle (Quest 22), they can attempt to banish Sorn through the portal as an alternative to killing him with the blessed blade — requiring a DC 20 combined Arcana + Athletics check but bypassing the blessed weapon requirement entirely. This is a deliberate failsafe for parties that lost the blade.
**Plot hook:** Destroying the anchors destabilises the portal but doesn't close it — that requires killing Sorn, whose life force sustains the gate. The party ascends to the throne room.

## The Finale (Level 11)

The party has reached 6th-level spells, cracking open the rakshasa's spell immunity. One well-placed Disintegrate, Harm, or other 6th-level spell can bypass Sorn's defences — but they have only one such slot, making the choice of when to use it dramatic and consequential.

### Quest 21: The Pretender's Court (Level 11)

**Overview:** The party ascends to the throne room to find Sorn has created a nightmarish mockery of a royal court. Illusory nobles applaud as he sits on a gilded throne, his true rakshasa form half-revealed — tiger-like features shimmering beneath a veneer of human beauty. He offers the party a final bargain — wealth, power, titles, the city itself — knowing that every moment of conversation lets the portal stabilise further. He uses his knowledge of the party's fears, desires, and past failures against them.
**Style:** Roleplay and psychological warfare before the final battle. Sorn addresses each party member individually, offering what they most want. He reveals truths they might not want to hear — the cost of their choices, the allies who died because of their decisions, the people they couldn't save. Players must resist his offers and see through his illusions. This is not a combat encounter — it is a test of the party's conviction.
**Creatures:** Illusory courtiers (not real but distracting). 2 rakshasa spawn (custom stat block: CR 6, AC 15, 75 HP, 2 claw attacks for 2d6+4, Limited Magic Immunity — immune to spells of 3rd level or lower, can cast Disguise Self and Detect Thoughts at will). These are lesser rakshasas newly arrived through the portal, weaker than true rakshasas but still dangerous. They are disguised as courtiers and reveal themselves when combat begins. Additionally, dominated NPCs (potentially including NPCs the party has befriended) are forced to fight — non-lethal takedown required.
**Adjusted difficulty:** 2 rakshasa spawn: base 4,600, multiplier x1.5 = 6,900 adjusted. **Hard** (level 11 threshold: Hard 8,800 / Deadly 12,800). The spawn's immunity to 3rd-level-and-lower spells is a constraint but not crippling — level 11 PCs have plenty of 4th and 5th-level slots. The dominated NPCs add complexity but are not meant to be lethal — just a moral complication. This encounter drains resources before the boss fight without being Deadly.
**Rewards:**
- **Loot:** The rakshasa spawn each drop a *Claw of the Rakshasa* — a curved dagger made from their own claws (1d6 piercing, counts as magical). These have no special properties but are worth 200 gp each to a collector of fiendish artefacts. They also drop *charm amulets* (the party should have a collection by now — turning these in after the campaign provides evidence for a city-wide de-charming effort).
- **Hidden find:** Behind Sorn's gilded throne, a DC 16 Investigation check reveals a hidden compartment containing Sorn's *Contingency Scroll* — a partially completed spell that would have teleported him to safety if the party had given him enough time during his monologue. The scroll is incomplete and cannot be used, but it confirms Sorn's cowardice beneath his bravado. More usefully, the compartment also contains a *Potion of Invulnerability* (resistance to all damage for 1 minute) and 500 gp in gemstones.
**Plot hook:** When the party refuses or attacks, Sorn drops all pretence. His human form tears away to reveal the full rakshasa — a towering tiger-headed fiend in gilded armour. The final battle begins.

### Quest 22: The True Face (Level 11)

**Overview:** The final confrontation with Vaelith Sorn. The throne room warps with fiendish energy as the unstable portal feeds him power. A blessed weapon must deliver the killing blow to prevent his return.
**Style:** Multi-phase boss battle.
- **Phase 1: The Deceiver.** Sorn fights with full rakshasa abilities plus legendary actions — charm, illusion, claw attacks. He creates illusory duplicates (3 at a time, DC 16 Investigation to identify the real one) and targets the wielder of the blessed blade specifically, attempting to disarm or charm them. He uses Dominate Person (DC 18) on martial characters. **Legendary actions (3/round):** Claw attack (1 action), Move without provoking (1 action), Cast Disguise Self or Detect Thoughts (2 actions).
- **Phase 2: The Tyrant.** At 100 HP or below, Sorn draws power from the destabilised portal, gaining flight (60 ft) and fire-based lair actions. He drops the illusions and fights with raw fury, revealing the resentment and desperation beneath his polished exterior. He screams about Mahavan, about being cast out, about proving them all wrong. **Lair actions (initiative 20):** Hellfire eruption (each creature in a 15-ft radius must make DC 16 Dex save or take 3d6 fire damage), Fiendish tremor (DC 15 Str save or knocked prone), or Charming pulse (DC 16 Wis save or charmed until the end of the creature's next turn).
- **Phase 3: The Fall.** At 50 HP or below, the portal begins collapsing, pulling everything toward it. The room cracks and tilts. Each round, creatures must make a DC 13 Strength save or slide 10 feet toward the portal. The party must deliver the killing blow while the ground crumbles and fiendish energy rages. If Brother Aldous is present and the blessed blade was lost, he can attempt to consecrate another weapon — but the party must protect him for 1 full round while he completes the ritual.
**Creatures:** Vaelith Sorn — enhanced rakshasa: AC 16, **200 HP**, 3 legendary actions per round, lair actions, Immune to spells of 6th level or lower. The party's single 6th-level spell slot can bypass his immunity — this is the dramatic crux. A well-timed Disintegrate (10d6+40 force damage, no save on hit) or Harm (14d6 necrotic) can be devastating but must be used at the right moment. Sorn summons 1 bearded devil (CR 3) per round via legendary action (costs 3 actions) to a maximum of 3 on the field at once.
**Adjusted difficulty:** Sorn alone (enhanced): ~12,000+ XP equivalent. With up to 3 bearded devils: base 14,100, multiplier x2 = 28,200 adjusted. **Deadly** (threshold 12,800). This is appropriate for a campaign-ending boss fight. The fight is meant to push the party to their limits. The blessed weapon requirement adds a secondary objective (protect the wielder) that elevates the tactical complexity beyond a simple DPS race.
**Rewards:**
- **Quest reward:** The **Gilded Throne** itself — Sorn's seat of power is a magical artefact worth 5,000 gp. It is cursed, however: anyone who sits in it for more than 1 minute must make a DC 18 Wisdom save or become convinced they deserve to rule Ashenmere. The curse can be removed with *Remove Curse* at 7th level, after which the throne becomes a mundane (but extremely valuable) piece of furniture. The Council of Guilds may wish to destroy it.
- **Loot:** Sorn's body dissolves, leaving behind his *Gilded Armour* — a suit of *+2 studded leather* that grants resistance to fire damage and advantage on Deception checks. His personal treasury (hidden beneath the throne) contains 3,000 gp in coin and gems, a *Rod of the Pact Keeper +2*, a *Stone of Good Luck*, and a sealed letter from Mahavan — written in Infernal, it disowns Sorn and mocks his ambition. The letter is historically significant and worth 1,000 gp to a scholar of the Nine Hells.
- **Hidden find:** If the party examines the collapsed portal (DC 18 Arcana), they recover a *Planar Compass* — a device that can detect planar rifts within 1 mile. It points toward any remaining fiendish influence in Ashenmere, allowing a thorough post-campaign cleanup. It also points toward other planes, making it valuable for future adventures. Beside the portal's remains, they find a personal journal of Sorn's — it details decades of careful planning and reveals the names and locations of three other rakshasas operating in cities across the Sword Coast. A campaign hook for the future.
**Resolution:** With Sorn slain by a blessed weapon, his charm magic unravels across the city. The portal collapses. Ashenmere is free but scarred — buildings burn, the Citadel is half-destroyed, and the political structure is in ruins. The epilogue deals with rebuilding: which guild leaders survived, how the council is reformed, and the party's newfound status as saviours of the city. The consequences of their earlier choices shape the new Ashenmere — did they save the journalist? Which guilds did they support? Who lived and who died? The Valiant Company raises a toast in their honour at the Dockmasters' tavern, and Brother Aldous — if he survived — begins rebuilding the Church of Tyr with renewed purpose.
